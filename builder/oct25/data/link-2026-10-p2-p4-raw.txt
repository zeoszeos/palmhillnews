Cover Photo
Palm Hill Country Club Golf Course Superintendent
Joe Clay - Keeper of The Course
I was grateful to spend time with Joe Clay before the busy Palm Hill golfing season arrived.
Being new here, I had no idea all the responsibility that belongs to a Country Club Golf Course
Superintendent. I knew that someone was mowing our course, before light, 6 out of 7 days,
and I could clearly see that someone was making sure that our course was well managed and
remained beautiful!
Joe started his life in St Louis, Missouri, and dutifully served in The United States Air Force.
Upon leaving the military, he earned his college degree in Golf Course Operations. Joe was
ready to put his knowledge to work and has continued to manicure golf courses since 1977.
As unpredictable as Florida weather can be, Joe has learned to be a controller of the elements.
Every day presents a new equation by balancing the irrigation required, with the chemical
applications required, with the weather conditions and with the City of Largo’s water
restrictions. Joe was kind enough to take the time to explain that throughout the year the ground
temperature determines the need for weed control, insect control, sprays and applications. He
added that protecting golfers from anything applied to the grass, demanded that all applications
were made late in the day. Nightly irrigation “waters down the course” and nothing is left
behind by morning Tee-Off Times.
Joe also explained the benefits and difÏculties of being an independent contractor. Maintaining
a positive relationship with the suppliers of needed materials and equipment is a top priority.
Keeping machines in the best working order when they are needed continuously is highly
challenging. All of Joe’s days have a strict schedule, but many days are reworked due to broken

Six days a week, each day begins between 6:20 and 6:30 am on the lawn mower. Leaving by

irrigation. Controlling start and stop times through a program on Joe’s phone ensures that all
fine details of this well-managed course are regulated with precision. Joe clarified that his years
in the job have earned him the greatest rewards. He gladly shares his passion for Golf Course
Management with everyone. Joe’s dedication to Palm Hill Country Club is obvious.
Thank you, Mr. Clay, for proudly serving our country and serving Palm Hill!
