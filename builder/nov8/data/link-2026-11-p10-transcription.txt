I’m currently in search of Palm Hill Residents who fit into what I believe is a rare category. I’m learning that I have landed in a perfect community that proves longevity. Reaching 90 and remaining able to do things you love is reality here. I want to discover some secrets to this possibility.

I found out about Tom Palladino by chance or maybe as Tom would say, “It was luck.”

Early one morning, I posed a question to one of the golfers traveling by my yard. “Good morning. Do you happen to know anyone here in the park that still plays golf and is 90 or over?” He dropped a name. “Yeah, I know somebody. Uh, uh, uh, Tom Palladino. He still gets out here nearly every day and he’s a great guy.” So, I waited for an hour or so and called Tom. And he answered the phone. Tom understood right away my interest in creating a story. Now 90, Tom was ready to share his perspectives on what life has taught him. We arranged for time together so I could hear about the years I may never get to see. If I can at least hear different perspectives of how the story goes, I’ll be grateful.

Before I could ask any questions, Mr. Palladino began by informing me that, “Life goes by fast, enjoy it.” He followed his opening wisdom with tidbits from stories that prove his theory. All his memories seem to hold a common thread which he stated as, “I’m just lucky.” His easy-going, kind-natured attitude was immediately admirable. I wanted to learn more. “I just live a simple life and that’s all there is to it.” If there’s something I want, I buy it and I don’t worry about things.” I thought the interview might be over.
