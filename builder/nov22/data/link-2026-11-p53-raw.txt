 WAY BACK WHEN
 Palm Hill history, a little at a time
 From your Palm Hill Communications team

 Forty years ago this month, on November 7, 1986, the State declared Home Owners of Palm
 Hill, Inc. ofÏcially a cooperative. That makes 2026 our 40th year owning our own park! So
 each month we’ll dig a little into our past, from Candy Armstrong Schmidt’s 1987 Story of
 Palm Hill Country Club and from the back issues of the LINK. See how you do!

 Did you know?
   • Our golf course opened to the public in late November 1970. You played five holes,
     walked west through the citrus grove with the mockingbirds singing, then played the
     last holes.
   • In 1970 the only phone in the park was in the sales ofÏce, and everyone’s mail went
     there too.
   • Snowbirds have always gotten a party. On November 4, 1979, “the season” opened at
     North Hall with champagne punch, coffee and cake... all free!

 Match it up! What did it cost?
   1. A round of golf, 1970 (with a coupon from the paper)       a. $144 a year
   2. Lot rent, 1970 (golf free forever!)                        b. a dime
   3. Showing up without your name tag, 1971                     c. $1
   4. Five Bingo cards, 1971                                     d. $40 a month
   5. A space for your boat or RV in the new compound, 1983      e. 50 cents

 10 years ago in the LINK (November 2016): the North Recreation Club’s Veterans Day
 program on November 11th at the North Clubhouse, with free coffee and donuts at 8:00, the
 program at 9:00 and a potluck at noon. And a reminder to snowbirds to check in with the
 ofÏce so your fob works at the pools and clubhouses. Some things never change! :)
