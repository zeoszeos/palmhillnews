V.        USE OF COMMON GROUNDS AND RECREATIONAL FACILITIES

     A. Use of the golf course is restricted to Members, lessees, or guests of either who pay greens fees. Annual golf
        memberships run from July 1 through June 30. Memberships are automatically terminated, without a refund, when
        a member is no longer a resident. A new Member is allowed to have a Golf Membership pro-rated for the first year
        only.

     B. The use of all common recreational equipment and facilities is under the control of the Board of Directors. Each of
        the Recreation Clubs is authorized to make available to users common use recreational equipment, furniture, kitchen
        utensils, and kitchen appliances that are in their custody and that are used in the respective clubhouses or recreation
        facilities located in the vicinity of each clubhouse or on the Park grounds. With the limited exceptions of the main
        assembly halls and kitchens in each clubhouse as prescribed in these Rules, the Recreation Clubs have no authority,
        entitlement, or responsibility to manage access to or use of any recreational facility or venue.

     C. Fees for the Use of Clubhouse Assembly Halls and Kitchens:
        1. The Recreation Clubs will jointly establish a single use fee schedule for the use of the Assembly Halls and
            Kitchens.
        2. Any changes to previously established fees must be submitted to the HOPH board prior to implementation.
        3. Refundable deposits and fees related to the set-up and clean-up of clubhouses may be charged for any event
            when a unit owner has exclusive use of the facility.
        4. Fees/deposits will be non-refundable when areas used are not properly cleaned.
                                                               3
          5. Fees may include refundable security deposits for the use of common recreational equipment or furniture made
             available to all users. No fees may be charged for the use of any other recreational equipment, facility, or venue
             except for the golf course.
          6. A single use contract will be completed and signed.
          7. No fee will be charged for the use of the clubhouse for a Palm Hill resident memorial service.
          8. Any group or club that sells tickets or charges an entry fee will need to check with the Rec Clubs as to whether
             they will have resale taxes due to the Florida Department of Revenue. Groups planning events should check
             with the Rec Club treasurer before planning the event to determine if the fee charged is enough to cover the
             cost of the taxes.

              The clubhouse, assembly hall and kitchen facilities may be used only by residents for special catered, immediate
              family or resident functions with the approval of the Recreation Club President.

              a. Groups outside of Palm Hill are not permitted to rent either clubhouse.
              b. Presentations explaining services, and programs available to assist seniors in maintaining their health or
                 aiding them with their safety or welfare may be permitted if approved by the Recreation Club officers.
              c. No alcoholic beverages are to be sold at any clubhouse function.
              d. The use of all common recreational equipment and facilities are under the control of the Board of Directors.
                 The authority of the Recreation Clubs is limited to management of the recreation hall and kitchen only.
              e. Large group size events/activities will be scheduled with respect to currently scheduled events and
                 activities.

      D. Liability Insurance: Each incorporated Recreation Club is required to maintain adequate liability insurance to protect
         its officers and members as well as HOPH, Inc. and all unit owners from risks associated with the conduct of the
         Recreation Clubs activities. Such insurance shall name HOPH, Inc. as an additional insured and a certificate of
         insurance shall be filed annually with HOPH, Inc.

      E. No person under sixteen (16) years of age will be allowed in or around the recreation areas unless accompanied
         by an adult.

      F. Garden Area. The garden area is a fenced and gated area located on the west side of Royal Palm Circle in the
         16th Avenue corridor. This area is restricted to gardening activity only by garden plot holders. There are over 30
         garden plots. Plots are available on a first come first serve basis through the office. The list of current plot holders
         and any waiting list is maintained by the Park Office. Access in and out of the area is managed by a combination
         lock with the combination being provided by the office to plot holders.

      G. The installation or planting of new trees and the removal of existing trees must have prior approval. Residents are
         responsible for tree trimming, shrubbery care, and weeding. All planters and gravel, stone, or pavers are to be kept
         free of weeds and trimmed. No shrubs or trees are allowed to be planted by residents within five (5) feet of the
         Park’s exterior white vinyl fence. Residents may not use the Park’s white vinyl fence or metal fencing for any
         purpose.
