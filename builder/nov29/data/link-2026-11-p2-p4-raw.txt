SMILING @ THE LADIES LUNCHEON
         olunteering for the first time appeared daunting. Sure, everyone says, “It’s easy, you’ll

    V    be fine.” I can be a bit of an overthinker and an over planner, so nothing is easy the
         first time. The instructions provided and the partner who picked me, as a trainee,
were what made my first experience co-hosting a Palm Hill Ladies Luncheon truly memorable.
For those of you who have hosting experience, I hope you continue to welcome such a fun
afternoon with friends.
  To me, it was extraordinary to spend 1 hour or less, 6 times in a month, asking ladies to
give me money. The instructions also stated that I needed to make a list with everyone’s name
and everyone’s phone number. Why would ladies who have only seen my face a few times be
willing to agree to such an arrangement? They were even bringing me extra money for their
friends. What kind of place is this? I’m collecting money from ladies who barely know me and
who are willing to trust me to hold $5 for them, plus give me their important information?
  They immediately trusted me to keep their money, like a bank, and return it in a few weeks
with some sort of reward for their trust? This is quite unusual and even a little suspicious. I
began to feel as if I was promising snowfall in Florida or selling snow to Eskimos, and they
were buying it. Maybe they imagined or read somewhere that I was putting in an order for a
youth elixir, and everyone was willing to give it a try. Either way, I suppose their $5 was not
too much to lose.

  Whatever was going on, the sweet system at Palm Hill works and it works well. After three
weeks, a total of 47 ladies signed up to have lunch with ME at a restaurant where I had never
been and I couldn’t tout. Oh, my goodness, I could never imagine such a gift. I’ve been living
here for six months and people were bringing me money and were willing to get dressed up and
are willing to drive to go to lunch with ME.
  I am grateful to everyone who took a chance and trusted in me.
  Well, then I found out that I followed my co-host’s directions and booked the restaurant
where everyone loves to go. And everyone arrived 30 minutes early and were ready to order
their well-tested selections. And then I learned that the restaurant was known and loved for
generously giving gift certificates to ensure that when the next new trainee comes through, they
can feel as special as I felt. There I was, going to lunch with 47 of my new generous neighbors,
who were more than willing to give me $5 just because they could tell, I just needed a few new
friends. Thanks for recognizing my neediness and thanks too for your kindness. Thanks for
your smiling faces! Thanks for showing me that at Palm Hill, love lives here!
  It all had nothing to do with me; I’m just a trainee.
