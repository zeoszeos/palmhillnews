VI.

HOMESITES AND CARE & MAINTENANCE OF PREMISES
A. All additions, renovations, or modifications to the home (“home” means the physical structure to include the carport
and driveway) or the unit’s grounds (“grounds” means the area outside the physical structure or footprint of the
home) must be approved by management in writing before work is commenced. This means any alteration of the
exterior appearance or features of the home or grounds and includes, but is not limited to: any exterior painting to
include the changing of the color of a driveway or home and its trim or shutters, siding a home, roof replacements
or coverings, room additions, cement work, planter enclosures, fencing of any kind, utility rooms or extensions,
installation of satellite dishes, carport extensions or expansions, installation of lampposts, tree plantings or
removals, installation of paver or cement patios or pathways, installation of filler materials over lawns, and
construction of golf cart pads, wood decks, and ramps. The Park Manager is authorized to approve resident work
requests and authorization is issued by the Park Manager. Approval is exercised by the Park Manager and this
authority may not be delegated. A written description, picture, and/or sketch of the proposed work or feature must
accompany the work request and, if applicable, must include approximate lot boundary lines. Acceptable colors for
painting or features are those that normally appear in residential neighborhoods but must be pre-approved before

being used. The resident is responsible for complete plans and the obtaining of any required permits from the City
or County for anticipated alterations. All work should be completed within 90 days of written approval, or as
extended by Park Manager. All contractors shall register at the Park Office.
B. Paver patios or wood decks at the rear of homes are permitted. These may be no longer than the width of the
home and may not exceed 12’ in depth from the back of the home. Prior approval is required. The allowable depth
and width may be decreased in the work request review process if lot lines, or physical features indicate that a
decreased depth or width is needed.
C. Pavers, pathways, or planter enclosures along the back or sides of homes are permitted but may not exceed six
(6) feet in width from the side of the home. The allowable width of six (6) feet may be decreased or increased in
the work request review process if the lot lines or physical features between homes indicates that a narrower or
wider width is suitable or necessary. A two (2) foot extension to widen the driveway with concrete and offset posts
is permitted. Prior approval is required.
D. Paver, stone, or mulch-material fill-in of the areas from the fronts of homes to the street is permitted. Prior approval
is required.
E. The installation or planting of new trees and the removal of existing trees must have prior approval. Residents are
responsible for tree trimming, shrubbery care, and weeding. All planters and gravel, stone, or pavers are to be kept
free of weeds and trimmed. No shrubs or trees are allowed to be planted by residents within five (5) feet of the
Park’s exterior white vinyl fence. Residents may not use the Park’s white vinyl fence or metal fencing for any
purpose.
F. Lawn ornaments and shrubbery which may be placed or planted should not impede grass cutting or other required
maintenance activities. The management reserves the right to require that lawn ornaments or shrubbery be
temporarily or permanently relocated or moved to allow for grounds maintenance or repairs. Hoses must be stored
clear of ground when not in use. If hoses are on the lawn when mowers are ready to mow, the grass will not be
mowed. Management is not responsible for damage to hoses left on the lawn or to sprinkler heads.
G. All homes and homesites must be maintained in a clean, orderly, and well-kept condition. This includes, but is not
limited to, the exterior appearance of the home, general upkeep, necessary repairs, painting, grounds maintenance,
and pest control. If a home or homesite is found to be in a state of disrepair or neglect, management reserves the
right pursuant to Paragraph 22 of the Master Form Exclusive Right of Possession Agreement to require the
shareholder to take corrective action. Should the shareholder fail to do so in a timely manner, management may
perform the necessary work or engage third party contractors to complete the required maintenance or repairs.
The shareholder will be responsible for the reasonable costs incurred. When park personnel perform such services,
a service fee of $50 per hour, per worker, shall be charged with a one hour minimum per occurrence.
H. Industrial containers, machinery, or discarded equipment and debris such as 5-gallon buckets or tire rims and the
like may not be used as planters, decorations, or lawn ornaments.
I.

No storage is permitted on the outside of or under homes. The single exception to this, no storage rule, is that one
(1) small garden locker or deck box not to exceed approximately 50 cubic feet in volume and six (6) feet in height
and designed for outdoor use is allowed. It must be securely placed against the side or back of a home and be of
a color that is compatible with the color of the home. Each resident is required to keep their premises, to include
the driveway and carport, free of debris. Patios, driveways, and carports may not be used for the installation or
storage of any appliances such as refrigerators, washers, or freezers and only items such as gas grills and patio
furniture which are intended and designed for outdoor use are allowed outside the enclosed spaces of the home
which include the porch and Florida room. Because of limited parking in the park, a unit carport must be used such
that it always allows space to park at least one automobile passenger vehicle.

J.

All trash, garbage, refuse, or recyclables must be placed in securely tied trash bags. Recyclables must be placed in
clear or transparent bags. Brush, trash, and recyclables must be placed at the curb side for collection on established
collection days. Trash or garbage must not be placed out the night before the collection day.
