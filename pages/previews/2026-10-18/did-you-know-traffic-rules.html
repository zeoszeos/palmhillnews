XI. TRAFFIC RULES

Let's face it - we have all seen certain rules completely ignored and the rule violated.

This week we would like to focus on traffic rules. The very first sentence in the rule states that the speed limit in the park is 15 MPH. We have all seen the rat race to get to the Seminole entrance or the 8th Ave entrance. Perhaps leaving home 5-10 minutes earlier or getting home a few minutes later would be okay.

Sections of the rule: The speed limit in the park is 15 mph. It's okay to go 15 mph and make a full stop at a stop sign. Further - Golf Carts and all motor vehicles shall not be operated after dark without tail lights and headlights. Golf carts and licensed mopeds shall not be operated by anyone under 16. Motorcycles are prohibited anywhere on Palm Hill property.. Please, all golf carts must be registered with the office. Most of all, be safe and be sure to follow the rules that are in place to protect us and follow a standard for the Park.

David Bates
