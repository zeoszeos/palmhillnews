MESSAGE FROM HENRY KLAIN, PRESIDENT OF THE BOARD - Jun, 2024

I hope this message finds everyone enjoying the warmer weather.

As an update, our Committees, the Board, and our management personnel have worked with vigor to ensure that enjoyment of our community remains paramount. This remains our collective focus as infrastructure issues are addressed, contracts for goods and services are of good value, and, most importantly, that we prepare for work related to the upcoming land lease renewal. Palm Hill continues to have a great future and we are working hard to sustain that.

I would like to address a recent petition that calls for an immediate tendering of the property management company contract. This is a matter that all Directors are aware of and sensitive to. While I am well aware of some valid issues and concerns, at the same time I understand the huge implications of such an undertaking. The reality is we have a binding contract until mid-2025 and will continue to evaluate whether this is a legitimate need. We also realize that further effort is required to put the association in a stronger position to be able to smoothly transition to a new property management company and exercise a proper procurement process. All things considered, I strongly do not believe it is in the best interests of the HOPH to move forward with this idea at this time. I believe we have a committed property management partner that is more than capable of overcoming the challenges, with everyone’s cooperation.

As we move forward through the next few months, we can expect outcomes that will continue to resolve infrastructure issues, improve the safety and reduced liability of residents, minimize costs wherever possible, and enhance administrative services.

Transparency remains a cornerstone of governance. Should a shareholder or resident wish to discuss any of the finer points or contribute constructive suggestions, I would be pleased to engage with you, as would other Board members.

Please benefit from the hard work, in progress and being accomplished, by all Committees.

Peace and good health!

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc.
