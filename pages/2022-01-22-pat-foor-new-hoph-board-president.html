President’s Corner-Jan 21, 2022

Thank you to the Palm Hill community for your support of your elected Board of Directors. Today was our Organizational Meeting to elect officers and appoint committee chairs/co-chairs. The results are:

President-Pat Foor
Vice President-Craig Mayer
Treasurer-Dave Kohli
Secretary-Carolyn Story

These officers make up the Executive Committee.

Committee chairs/co-chairs are:

Building & Grounds Co-Chairs, Bob Newport and Chuck Silcox
Communications Chair, Dan Hannafin
Finance Chair, Dave Kohli
Golf Chair, Ben Brubaker and Board Liaison, Carolyn Story
Planning Co-Chairs, Dave Kohli and Henry Klain
Real Estate Chair, Carolyn Story
Rules & Regulations Co-Chairs, Henry Klain and Dan Avery

Each committee will prepare a vision statement with goals they hope to accomplish in 2022 and beyond. This is a beginning framework for their work.

I am excited to lead this team of dedicated individuals as we work on your behalf to govern Palm Hill this year. Please contact one of us if you would like to be considered for one of the committees. We will vote to approve committee members at our Feb 9th webinar board meeting.

We want to listen to and communicate effectively with you, our shareholders. We must be respectful in our dialogue as we discuss issues. Much of our preparation for board action begins at the committee level. Talk with your board members and committee chairs/co-chairs to share your thoughts. Your comments and suggestions may also be shared with the Business Office to pass on to us. We are here to listen to resident input. That, along with accurate facts and very specific recommendations, assists board members at decision time.

We will continue with webinar/Zoom committees and board meetings and viewing on Channel 732. Meetings are accurately recorded, can be viewed anywhere with WiFi at your leisure through our website and be done in a safe environment. Following board approval the minutes are posted on our website. Training will be offered for those wishing to learn more about this technology.

We begin this year on firm financial footing. Working together, we will keep Palm Hill the vibrant community we all enjoy today and prepare for a bright tomorrow.

Respectfully,

Pat Foor, President

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email on Saturday, Jan 22, 2022, at 11:11 AM ET.
