Palm Hill Country Club

Weekly Manager Report — September 18, 2026

Good Afternoon All,

- Inliner was out on Monday to run cameras through the storm sewer on Fern. They were back out again yesterday for more measurements of the pipe. We are waiting on follow-up information on this, but anticipate that it will need to be lined and a full-depth patch installed for that section of the roadway. Inliner's engineer is looking at the video, and we hope to have recommendations for pipe and box repair on Wednesday.

- The tractor repair company was out on Monday to evaluate the tractor. They determined that the wire connecting the battery was bad, and they were out to complete the repair on Wednesday.

- A resident questioned the lack of a permit for new plants being installed in their yard. The existing permit was for grass and sod, allowing 30 days. Because the watering restrictions state "lawns and landscaping," we have added a permit to water new plants for 14 days. We don't want to prevent people from planting new plants, but we also don't want to allow them the same 30 days permitted for grass and sod, as the research indicates 14 days is sufficient.

- Maintenance for the floor scrubber machine has been scheduled for Monday. The company that we purchased it from used to have a local location in Clearwater, but they closed that branch. We found a company based in St. Pete and will be paying half of what we would have paid to have the original company come out.

- Universal Controls was out today, and the new meter was installed on the north well.

- Attached is an upcoming legal update webinar open to Board members. These are provided annually to update us on new laws that have been passed. They are always highly informative. If you are available, I encourage you to attend.

- The engineer for the transformer has confirmed that she will be on-site on Monday to take photos of the area and anticipates having her design completed by next Friday. We will receive the next steps at that time.

- Vendor installing doors in the south kitchen has confirmed receipt of the deposit check. They have ordered the doors and anticipate receiving them on Monday, and will install the new doors next week.

- Water fountains were received today to replace the ones in each clubhouse. The Drain team will be doing the installation. We do not yet have a date scheduled.

- The south hot tub had a smoking electrical panel on Monday when the crew came in to do their maintenance. We shut down the pumps and closed the hot tub. D'Andrea Electric came out and made repairs, and the hot tub reopened on Tuesday.

- We had another car hit the fence on Seminole on Thursday. Maintenance will get the fence repaired on Monday. We have the insurance information for the people involved in the accident, and I will file a claim. I followed up with the tow company that had previously struck it, as we have not yet received that check.

Source: Megan Leftwich, Weekly Manager Report
