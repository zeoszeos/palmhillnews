Weekly Manager Update

From the Palm Hill office · Megan Leftwich, On-Site Manager

Friday, Jul 31, 2026
← Back

Good Afternoon Everyone,

  • We had some excitement this week with a car running off the road on Sabal and into the fence at the golf shed between 58 and 60 Sabal. The two occupants of the vehicle got out and ran from the scene. A piece of the damaged fence struck the valve atop the reclaimed water lines, knocking it off along with the connected PVC. I have the police report number and am checking with Largo PD to get a copy of the report as soon as it becomes available. Both Anthony and Joe Clay have been advised to make all necessary repairs and to provide me with all receipts associated with this. Once I have a copy of the police report, we can determine if there is insurance to file a claim.
  • We have requested a meeting with BrightView regarding the ongoing string-trimming issues that we are having. Jess has been reporting them, and they brought out a special crew to address the areas, but the work is still lacking. We have requested a meeting the day after the service so we can drive and point out the specific areas. There are some areas throughout the community, but primarily along Pindo West, that the reports most often concern.
  • Quotes were obtained for replacing the exterior kitchen doors in the south clubhouse due to the existing doors rusting and the hinges on the bottom coming out. The low bid was Rosell. Since this is within Henry’s authorization amount ($4,005.81), he has signed it, and it was returned to the vendor today. They have provided an invoice for the deposit which has been submitted for payment.
  • Anthony has been working to update all the signs in the community, and there are still some more to address.
  • I’ve also asked Anthony to address the lights in the central area in front of the south clubhouse and by the shuffleboard courts/golf cart parking in the north. These lights are in extremely bad shape. The one on the north side of the south parking lot has the electrical zip tied to it, is rusting, and the light is attached to a piece of wood at the top. He will research the options and make a recommendation, which I will share with you once he has completed his research.
  • Based on recommendations from United and after speaking with Linda Keehl and Anthony, I have set up a return on the freezer that was installed in the south kitchen. The technicians believe the door isn’t sealing properly because the manufacturer installed an incorrectly sized door on the unit. We are going to replace it with one of the white upright freezers, similar to the one installed in the north kitchen. Home Depot has arranged for pickup and will refund the entire amount. The white freezers are approximately half the cost of the version that is being sent back.
  • In addition to this, the refrigerator in the south kitchen appears to have a bent bar between the two doors. This is preventing the gaskets from touching in this area, allowing condensation to build up and drip down. I am looking into replacing this refrigerator because it cannot be repaired.
  • The leak in the north clubhouse has been determined to be the AC. Anthony went into the attic and checked it over, initially thinking a drain line was clogged. However, the issue is the ductwork. It is generating condensation that is dripping onto the ceiling. We have contacted the AC vendor to come take a look and make a recommendation. We are both unsure as to why this would be an issue now, as nothing has recently changed with the air conditioners.

Hope you all have a great weekend, everybody is healthy, and there are no more crazy northern weather events.

Sincerely,
Megan Leftwich, CMCA, AMS
On-Site Manager
