97½ and Still Chasing Birdies Instead of Birthdays

Sunday, Oct 4, 2026Listen
← Back

Written by Carolyn M. Story, as told by Mary Leslie · Golf Advisory Committee

There are no mulligans needed—Mary Leslie has aced nearly a century! Mary and her husband, Herb, came to Florida in 1987 and settled in Palm Hill in 1988. They first rented on Thatch Way and Royal Palm before purchasing their home on Cane Palm in 2002. Mary began learning golf in 1988, with Herb as her instructor. He had a deep love for the game, and the couple decided it was something they could share together.

My husband, Richie, and I invited them to a golf outing we organized in Sebring, Florida. There, Mary and Herb met many golfers—on the course and in the hospitality room—marking the start of a lifetime of golfing friendships and activities. Mary recalls raising money for the new Pro Shop through spaghetti dinners, cookie sales, and an ongoing 50/50 draw.

Mary has held various positions with the Palm Hill Ladies Golf League, serving as Secretary and as President from 2007 to 2009. She was also Secretary of the South Recreation Club. Mary is someone who contributes to her community wherever she is needed.

Mary and Herb reshaped the annual Ladies’ and Men’s Golf Awards Dinner by combining the two events into one. The new dress code called for suits and tuxedos alongside cocktail dresses. This change complemented an upscale dinner and a well-rehearsed awards presentation honoring the winners of each division.

Mary’s first hole-in-one came at Rowley Country Club in Rowley, Massachusetts, on Oct 7, 1991. Since then, she has scored ten more at Palm Hill. Her favorite hole at Palm Hill is hole #5. During their years splitting time between Massachusetts and Palm Hill as snowbirds, the couple once returned from the North and, even before unpacking, headed straight to the golf course—where Herb scored a hole-in-one on hole #3. It surprised him more than anyone, especially since they played very little golf while up north.

Mary has a few guiding thoughts about the game: perseverance is essential; patience matters; always be ready when it’s your turn; keep play moving; save the chatter for the clubhouse afterward; and, above all, have fun.

Golf has enriched Mary and Herb’s social lives and given them lasting friendships, though many of those friends have since passed away. Mary admires the spirit and enthusiasm of the younger women who now hold office and play with the Palm Hill Ladies Golf League. Mary thanks God for her good health and her continued physical flexibility.

“My scores have increased, which is understandable; however, sometimes my inability to play a better game is frustrating.”

Mary’s continued passion is the scorecard that keeps her forever in the game. These days, at ninety-seven and a half, Mary isn’t counting the years—she’s only counting the fairways.
