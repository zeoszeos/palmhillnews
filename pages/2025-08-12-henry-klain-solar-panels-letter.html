Dear Shareholders,

On Jan 22, 2025, your Board of Directors voted to advance a proposal we believe will deliver lasting financial benefits for every shareholder, the installation of solar panels on the clubhouse roofs.

This project is expected to dramatically reduce Palm Hill’s electricity charges from the moment it is installed, cutting operating expenses in the very next budget year. With utility costs continuing to rise, renewable energy provides a rare opportunity to take control of a major expense, and keep more money in our community.

The financial case is compelling: our conservative estimates show a break-even point in only 6 years, followed by substantial net savings for the remaining 14–19 years of the system’s 20–25 year lifespan. These savings will help keep maintenance fees affordable for everyone. Adding to the urgency, federal tax credits that lower our upfront cost will expire on Dec 31, 2025.

Because this project is considered a material alteration, shareholder approval is required. We have scheduled a special meeting for Friday, Sep 26, 2025, where a majority of votes (in person or by proxy) will be needed to authorize the project. The official notice and voting materials will follow in a separate mailing.

This proposal is the result of months of research, planning, and cost analysis to ensure it delivers immediate value and positions Palm Hill for a more sustainable future. We will provide more details shortly, including how to vote electronically or by paper ballot.

Your support will be critical. By voting in favor, you will be helping Palm Hill lower operating costs, protect against rising utility prices, and make a strategic investment that benefits every shareholder for decades to come.

On behalf of Palm Hill Board of Directors, let’s work together to secure this opportunity and keep Palm Hill strong, sustainable, and affordable.

Henry Klain, President
