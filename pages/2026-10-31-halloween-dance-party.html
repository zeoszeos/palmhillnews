Halloween Dance Party

Saturday, October 31, 2026 • 6:15 PM • North Clubhouse

Doors and seating
Doors open at 6:15 PM. There is no assigned seating ahead of time. The September 23 notice said dancing runs 7:00–10:00 PM with music by Sage, and the dance is BYOB.
Tickets
Tickets are sold out as of October 8. Some may become available if they are not picked up and paid for, so stay tuned. The September 23 notice listed $10 tickets at the Monday and Wednesday coffees.
Treats and costumes
Bring a treat to share for table-to-table trick-or-treating and the costume contest.
Haunted Library
The Haunted Library is $2, or free with your dance ticket. More details are on the bulletin boards in the clubhouses.
