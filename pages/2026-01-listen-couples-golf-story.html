Going Strong: Couples Golf Group Celebrates 17-Year History

Jan 1, 2026Listen
← Back

By Carolyn Story with Mac McElhinney

Couples Golf began in 2008, 17 years ago, during the “Yes/No” history of Palm Hill. This was a contentious time when a proposal to purchase “The Park” from the Taylor Family divided shareholders.

Longtime Palm Hill resident and golfer Mac McElhinney recalled that within the Bandits golfing group, it got to a point that the “Yesses” and “Nos” sat separately on the Pro Shop porch in the morning, not even speaking to each other.

Mac came up with the thought that playing golf could alleviate this somewhat and get longtime friends past this uncomfortable situation and speak to each other again.

Mac and Carolynn Flaherty’s original plan was to play at East Bay Golf Course, but that was changed to Palm Hill since everyone had a membership here. Everyone gathered afterward for dinner at 4 p.m. at Cody’s Restaurant in Largo. Wednesday was the day chosen because Cody’s offered two-for-one fajitas that day.

The rules were:

  1. You could talk about politics.
  2. You could talk about religion.
  3. You could talk about sports.
  4. YOU COULD NOT TALK ABOUT PALM HILL.

The original group consisted of Mac McElhinney and Carolynn Flaherty, Fred and Norma Ashwell, E.R. and Ruth Vissman, Phil and Rhoda Magistro, Herb and Mary Leslie, and Richie and Carolyn Story.

Originally, teams were selected by placing the names in a bag and then selecting randomly. However, as time went on, and Couples Golf grew in numbers, teams were assigned by those volunteering to take charge of the pairings. They never imagined during the formative years that this event would expand in popularity and interest to the numbers involved today. In mid-winter as many as 100 to 120 people play golf together in scramble format on Thursday mornings. (Sign-up is available at the Pro Shop.)

Couples Golf 17th Anniversary Event

Eighty-four golfers, plus some former “Couples” golfers who can no longer play golf, attended the Dec 4 celebration, which was sponsored by Jim and Sandy Jones, Carolyn Story and Howard Sneed, and Terri and Sean MacGilvray, who all donated the food. Volunteers who also donated condiments and did the cooking: Randy Mitchell, Jeff Wesolowski and Tom “Pickles” Locher.

Today’s “Couples” golfers thanked and celebrated Mac and Carolynn for initiating “Couples Golf” with the intent to bring friends back to the core and realize that differences of opinions are acceptable behavior and continuous friendship and respect are paramount in all relationships.
