President’s Corner

As we prepare to begin 2024 I would like offer my sincere apologies for any waysI have contributed to the increased negativity we are now experiencing in our community. We want to promote a more peaceful climate where we can enjoy all that Palm Hill has to offer. Let us each look inward to find ways we can work more collaboratively to solve our issues and find more positive paths forward. Web blasts and the message board will not publish or endorse negative content.

May the coming year be a good one for Palm Hill and the 2024 board of directorsas they seek solutions to the challenges before us.

May good health and happiness carry you through 2024,

Pat Foor, President
Home Owners of Palm Hill, Inc.

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email on Saturday, December 30, 2023, at 7:56 PM ET.
