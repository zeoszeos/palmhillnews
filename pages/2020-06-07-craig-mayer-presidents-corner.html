Presidents Corner

I find myself having to send this message in order to address a misconception about land ownership in the Park and to stop the spreading of false information.

A mistaken belief exists that the land known as the 16th Avenue corridor which includes the garden and dog walk area of the Park is owned by the City of Largo. It is not owned by the City. It is leased by HOPH, Inc. and owned by the Taylor Family as is the remainder of the Cooperative’s property. The City’s property interest in the corridor area consists of only easements. Documentation confirming these property interests and relationship is available from the City or the Park office.

In an email on 25 May to the Board Secretary board member Mr. Hanke stated that this land was chosen as the site for holding the recent unapproved memorialservice because it was believed that activities conducted on that land would notbe subject to the Board’s authority. Certainly not by chance, this mistaken belief about ownership was chosen to be included in a shareholder speech on June 1stduring the Covid-19 workshop portion of the Board meeting. Additionally, a number of equally mistaken shareholders have trumpeted this belief on the website message board.

If this false information is relied upon and continues to be spread, it could serve as an invitation to the general public to come into the Park to make use of property they have no right to use. Similarly, it could as well mislead residents into making unauthorized uses of the land.

Any events intended to be held in this area of the Park, as all events in common areas of the Park, are subject to Board authority and before being conducted require approval.

Thank you for supporting Palm Hill,

Craig Mayer
President
HOPH, Inc.

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email on Sunday, June 7, 2020, at 8:18 AM ET.
