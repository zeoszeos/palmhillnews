Welcome to 2024!

Success will not be a solo journey and my wishes are to promote peace, goodwill and co-operation so we all can concentrate on good governance and collective, positive outcomes this year. Shareholders and residents expect and deserve it!

The Home Owners of Palm Hill (HOPH) Board passed the appointments of Committee Co-chairs and Board liaisons at the Jan 26th board meeting. Thank you to all who have volunteered their services!

Our next step is for our Co-chairs to select committee members, which will be confirmed at the Board meeting scheduled for Feb 7th. The selection process will be completed at that point and the committees can then start their work as advisors to the Board. This is important work that keeps Palm Hill running smoothly.

To start, our priorities are infrastructure renewal (sewers, water pipes, pools, etc.), compliance with our Rules & Regulations, improving communications (e.g. website, emails), the Taylor land lease reset, and the successful introduction of a new park management software application (Vantaca – more to come). We’re looking forward to collaborating on these with all stakeholders!

Most importantly, I’m available to you to converse/communicate by whatever means you choose, please feel free to reach out to me.

Cheers,
Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc.
