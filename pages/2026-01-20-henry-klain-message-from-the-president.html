Although the number of Palm Hill shareholders raising concerns is extremely small, the ongoing personal criticism and accusations directed toward volunteers, committee members, and board members, individuals who generously contribute their skills and personal time, has become increasingly serious and concerning.

Information related to financial transactions and operational matters is available and transparent. Shareholders who have questions or concerns are encouraged to access these materials and seek clarification through appropriate channels.

The Board of Directors, together with our management company, RPM, will continue to uphold our fiduciary responsibilities and remain fully accountable and transparent in all operations.

We respect every shareholder’s right to freedom of speech. At the same time, we ask that this freedom be exercised with consideration for those who volunteer their time for the well-being of Palm Hill. Disseminating misinformation rather than seeking accurate information and answers is neither helpful nor reflective of responsible shareholder conduct.

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email on Tuesday, Jan 20, 2026.
