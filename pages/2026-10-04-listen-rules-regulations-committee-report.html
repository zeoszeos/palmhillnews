Rules and Regulations Committee Report

Tap Read Report Aloud to begin.

A meeting of the Rules and Regulations Committee occurred on August 10, 2026 at 1 pm.
It was reported that 2 members have resigned, Elizabeth Herr and Rebecca Webb.
There was discussion on the number of dogs in the park, and the requirements for them being authorized.
It was reported that so far, any animal reported to the office has the required documentation.
It was asked if the office could report to the individual reporting a suspect animal, that the animal had the required paperwork.
We would need to be careful with this, because of HIPPA rules and Privacy concerns.
Megan recommends that we clear all ESA request through the Attorney since the rules on ESA’s are changing, although in the past, if the required paperwork accompanied the request, the Attorney said we should allow the animal.
It was also discussed if we should require an Annual Registration to make sure the animal was still allowed.
The discussion covered the possible penalties for falsely claiming the animal was a Service Animal.
It was reported that a few individuals had taken their dogs to the pool to let them swim,

THIS IS STRICTLY NOT ALLOWED !!!

This topic was Tabled to allow additional information to be gathered.

The Replacement of removed trees was brought forward.
There are requirements for trees to be replaced in certain circumstances.
This topic requires further investigation and consideration.
HOPH currently doesn’t have a rule on this, but there are City and County Rules to be looked into

The next topic was a Violation and Penalties section to be added to the Golf Code of Conduct.
This subject will be covered further during the current Board Meeting agenda.

Next up was clarification of the rules on people staying more than 30 days.
This will be a topic further in the current agenda.
The last topic was discussion on Weapons being carried in Common Areas and at Social Events.
This was Tabled for further Investigation and Discussion.
The next Rules and Regulations meeting is set for October 12, 2026 at 1 pm.
The meeting was adjourned.
