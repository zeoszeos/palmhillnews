President’s Message: 2025 Officers and Committees

Friday, Jan 24, 2025Listen
President's Message Archives → · All Archives →

Today on Jan 24, 2025 the board of directors held an organizational meeting which focused on appointing the executive portion of the board. The Vice President will be David Bates, Gordie Sanford will continue as Treasurer and Terri MacGilvary as Secretary. I am also grateful for the support of the board to continue as President.

I would also like to congratulate those elected to join us on the board. Terri MacGilvary, Bob Newport, Dan Russom and David Bates. I also thank those that ran for election and appreciate the efforts to do so. At this time the board its fully staffed at 9 directors.

In order for us to get to work and have our committees operational as soon as possible, the board has set a date of Jan 29th, 2025 at 10:00am to convene and make appointments to committees and the chairs as required.

With this in mind, this is a call for "expressions of interest" to anyone who wishes to contribute to the following committees:

  • 1) Finance
  • 2) Planning & Environmental
  • 3) Building & Grounds
  • 4) Communications
  • 5) Rules & Regulations
  • 6) Compliance
  • 7) Golf
  • 8) Real Estate
  • 9) Past Presidents Committee

We can always add to committee members in the future, however in the meantime making our committees operational as quickly as possible is imperative.

Those who have contributed as a member of a committee and so wish to continue, please confirm with your committee chairs.

Those who are interested in either chairing committees or contributing as a committee member, I would appreciate your interest by e-mail or telephone/text [personal contact details removed for the web archive].

Henry Klain
