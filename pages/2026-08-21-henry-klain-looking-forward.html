Shareholders & Residents:

Contemplating our community’s past and future, I have spent the first decade of my retirement reflecting on accomplishments in life and, more specifically, what makes Palm Hill Country Club so special.

Palm Hill is more than a place to live. It is a retirement cooperative that has served that purpose for decades. It is one of the reasons Darlene and I, along with countless others, chose to spend our retirement years here, with friends, neighbours and like-minded people who share the same appreciation for this stage of life. We are not alone in that journey.

Some have said that Palm Hill attracts those seeking a resort-like environment at a bargain price. Reflecting on that observation, I believe there is considerable truth to it. Our monthly maintenance fee, combined with the amenities, social activities, physical facilities and onsite services, represents remarkable value in today’s marketplace.

But perhaps we underestimate what Palm Hill has truly accomplished.

Much of what makes this community successful has been built and sustained by shareholders who voluntarily contribute their time, knowledge, experience and expertise for the benefit of others. That contribution is difficult to put a price on...it is, quite simply, priceless.

As we contemplate Palm Hill’s future, we should never lose sight of this unique character. We are a cooperative built by shareholders, for shareholders. That special status is what separates Palm Hill from a commercial enterprise, or a Home Owners Association (HOA) and is one of the greatest assets we have.

We should protect it, value it and ensure that it continues for generations of Palm Hill shareholders to come.

Continued health and happiness,

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc
