Mar 23, 2021

The second full meeting of your new board was completed yesterday, with reports heard from our Officers, the Park Manager, and all the Committees. We also dealt with many new business agenda items in a fairly reasonable fashion, and time was taken after the meeting adjourned to allow comments from a few residents. In total our meeting lasted nearly 2 ½ hours, which I think is too long for all involved. In addition to the time change for future meetings to 2:00 pm instead of 3:00 pm, I will also be looking for ways to reduce the length of time these meetings take.

Board Meeting Results – The results of this meeting were as follows:

1. Financial: The Treasurer gave his financial report for Feb, which was approved for publication and inclusion in the Treasurer’s corner of the website. For the 8 months ended Feb 28, 2021 we have earned an operating profit of $17,370 compared to a budgeted profit of $38,900 and we have a total of $1,395,161 in our bank accounts ($82,282 in Operating, $1,154,666 in Reserve, and $158,213 in Savings). We are on target to meet our operating budget for the 2020-2021 fiscal year.

2. Budget 2021-22: Final approval was given to the budget that was previously mailed to all residents. It calls for a $17/household/month increase to $396 effective Apr 1, 2021.

3. Insurance: Coverage for the year starting Mar 23, 2021 through to Mar 22, 2022 was approved for a total cost of $147,825, with $132,567 of this amount pre-paid in Mar from our Savings account, which will be restored in 12 equal monthly payments from the operating account as funds become available.

4. Reserve Study: A new Reserve Study was approved to replace the one done in 2018 at a cost of $2,925. Our tradition is to do a study every 3 years, to keep our park up to date on these future costs, and fully fund the Reserve account to meet our needs 30 years into the future. We also intend to make this document the cornerstone of our emerging program to replace our aging infrastructure.

5. Online Voting: Our park is now authorized to use electronic voting, in addition to the traditional “in person” or “by mail” options. Residents wishing to vote in this manner will have to “opt in” to the system, and therefore be excluded from voting in person or by mail. In the weeks that follow we will choose and sign a contract with a software supplier and work out the details of how our election procedures will need to be changed. Note: electronic voting is completely optional for all residents, the system is very easy to use, and it will save us money by reducing postage, printing, and handling costs.

6. Flea Spraying: A one year contract was approved for 2 sprays for fleas, including every home at a cost of $2,192. It is somewhat of an experiment to see what degree of reduction in the flea numbers we attain. After one year the renewal of this program will be evaluated. Residents will be advised about the date and time of each spray, and precautions will need to be taken and pets kept inside for one hour after each spray.

7. Roof Covers: Approval was given to build two aluminum roof covers in the sanitation field at a cost of $6,434 + permitting, using funds from the Savings account. One will be 10’x12’ and cover the area where volunteers work to recycle the metal, and the other will be 9’x20’ beside the recycling compactor to cover the area where our staffer Bob Dorsey spends a lot of time.

8. Compliance Committee: The re-establishment of this committee and the naming of its members was approved. Its role is to provide an appeal option for residents who wish to appeal a fine or sanction they have received from the board for an alleged infraction of the park’s rules.

9. Future Meetings: Our next scheduled meeting is Apr 19, 2021 at 2:00 pm. Potential agenda items include more about the On Line Voting system, the Reserve Study, the Lawn Mowing contract and other issues that the board is working on. Apr 9, 2021 is the deadline we have set for ourselves to produce a draft agenda for this meeting. If you have an issue you would like addressed, contact any board member prior to the Apr 9 date. For planning purposes, May 17, 2021 at 2:00 pm was the date set for the following meeting.

Town Hall Meetings – We held town hall meetings on Mar 4 to discuss Covid, and Mar 18 to discuss on line voting and the flea spraying program. By all accounts they appear to have been successful and well received. Valuable information was received to aid our decision making. Thanks to Pat, Ralph, and Megan for their efforts, and a special thanks to Chuck Silcox for the excellent presentation of the on line voting initiative. More town hall meetings will be held.

Priorities – With the budgeting and insurance issues now behind us, the board will focus on the longer term issues of on line voting, a new reserve study leading to infrastructure replacement, and the possibility of building pickleball courts. Several other shorter term issues are also on the go.

Home on 718 Sugar – Last week we concluded negotiations with the estate, received title to the property, changed the locks and inspected the home. On or before our next scheduled board meeting we will determine the best approach for cleaning up and selling the property. We have an Account Receivable on our books for about $15,000 from unpaid maintenance fees and settlement funds, and we have expended about $12,000 in legal fees protecting our interests, for a total sunk cost of about $27,000 in the property. Our goal would be to at least recover these costs through a sale.

Thanks for your support.

Dave Kohli, President
