February 17, 2021

My Start as your President – The first two weeks of being your president have been exciting, interesting and overwhelming at times, but also pretty satisfying. I believe we have laid some of the groundwork for a prosperous 2021. For all those who have emailed with good wishes, I thank you, and for those who emailed with comments and/or questions but have not received a reply, my apologies – please don’t be offended. I have received more correspondence lately than anytime I can remember in my life. All I can tell you, is that I am digesting it all, and trying to fit everything into an overall plan.

Board Meeting Results – We held our first full board meeting yesterday, and the results were as follows:

1. Reports: We heard update reports from our officers, committee chairs, and the park manager, including presentation and approval of the January financial statements, that will be available in the Treasurer’s corner shortly.

2. 60 Thatch West: A Variance was approved allowing for the replacement of a new home at this address.

3. Budget 2021-22: The budget was pre-approved with a $17/household/month increase, including the use of up to $135,000 from our Savings account to pre-pay our insurance premiums on March 23/21. This allows the office to mail it out to all residents, by Friday.

4. Committees: A list of all new committee members was approved. In case you didn’t know, committee chairs were elected by the board at the 2nd Organizational meeting, and the Chairs then choose the members for their own committees. If you weren’t chosen for a committee that you expressed interest in being on, please contact the committee Chair/Co-chair. New members can be added by board vote at any time.

5. Office Renovations: New tiling has been purchased to replace the flooring in the offices and boardroom. The board approved some minor renovations, and changes to the offices that will be done at the same time in June when the office is less busy. Contact Carolyn Story if you would like more details.

6. Future Meetings: Our next scheduled meeting is March 22, 2021 at 3:00 pm. Already on the agenda are Final Approval of the Budget and On Line Voting. Several other issues are under consideration to be added before the March 12, 2021 deadline we have set for ourselves to produce a draft agenda for this meeting. If you have an issue you would like addressed, contact any board member prior to the Mar 12 date. For planning purposes, April 19, 2021 at 3:00 pm was the date set for the following meeting.

Future Initiatives – We are now in the process of getting our committees functioning, determining our objectives and setting dates for first meetings. As President, I will take an active role in encouraging committees to address issues that support our board’s objectives and concerns. To that end, I have asked our Vice-President to formulate a process, and set in motion a Town Hall meeting at which residents could recommend issues that they would like our board to pursue. Such a meeting could also identify a specific topic that the board would like to gather resident’s opinions on.

Board Meetings and Conduct of Board Members – At our first meeting we used the guidelines agreed to at our 2nd Organizational meeting, and it appears to have worked reasonably well. There are some kinks to work out, but I think we are on the right path. Thanks for your support.

Dave Kohli, President
