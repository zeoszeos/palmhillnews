Vendor Meetings & Maintenance Updates

  1. We had a car hit the fence on Seminole Blvd on Aug 15 and again on Sep 17. All repairs have been made. FDOT has been contacted again regarding the accidents. FDOT added lines through the intersection for the southbound lanes on Seminole Blvd. in Feb. We have received a check for repairs from the Aug 15 accident and have submitted a claim for the Sep 17 accident.
  2. The new air conditioner for the north clubhouse has been installed and is fully up and running.
  3. Sewer work in the north has been completed. There are a few final cleanup issues that are being addressed. We are waiting on an invoice from Inliner for the remaining unpaid work.
  4. The roadway on Fern between Queen Palm St. and Sea Oats Dr. remains closed off. Inliner has run a camera and submitted a quote to line the storm sewer section in the area and around the manhole. The quote does not include repair work to the roadway.
  5. Ice machine relocation has been completed in the north clubhouse.
  6. The electrical panel in the south pool area had a short in it. The hot tub was closed on Sep 14 for repairs and reopened on Tuesday, Sep 15.
  7. The purple pipe paint job has been completed in both the cross between north and south and the golf shed.
  8. A tractor repair was completed on Sep 16. A wire had gone bad and was replaced.
  9. New water fountains have been installed in both the north and south clubhouses. The new fountains have filters and bottle-filling stations. Drain Team completed the installation on Sep 29, 2026. Maintenance will address the framing and wall issues caused by the size difference of the new machines.
  10. The floor scrubbing machine had maintenance performed on Sep 21. They checked all the components and replaced a leaking hose. All other components are in good working order. Training for how to use the machine was completed on Sep 24.
  11. The meter on the north well was replaced on Thursday, Sep 18. It is now up and running, and the calibration has been reported to SWFWMD.
  12. The sunshade installation at the north pool has now been completed with the installation of the sunshades on Sep 21 and Sep 22.
  13. The vendor for the FOB system was onsite on Sep 24. The gate at the sanitation field was not communicating with the cloud. They have made repairs, and the system is up and running correctly again.
  14. Volunteers finished moving books from the south to the north and took the shelves off the wall. Maintenance took the shelves out and down to the maintenance area. A table and chairs have been placed in the room and it has already been used for an orientation.

Administrative Updates

  1. Permits for the grease traps have been renewed, as well as the POCTS (Privately-Owned Collection & Transmission System). We have received 2 of the 3 and are waiting on the 3rd to arrive. Largo inspected the grease traps the week of Aug 31, 2026, and all passed.
  2. We had a question come to the office about permits to water in new plants. We reviewed the documentation that is in place. We reviewed the online watering recommendations for new plantings. A new permit was created that allows owners to water new plants for 14 days, and it can be obtained at the office. Please remember to display these in the front window of your home so they are visible from the street.
  3. The Communications committee members organizing the Meet the Candidates night met on Sep 22 to discuss their plans and strategize ways to improve the process. We sat with them to answer questions about dates and other aspects of the event.
  4. Office staff have been scanning files for the Association in order to make them digital instead of paper. Additionally, the files are being organized for ease of access. The anticipated completion for this project is next week.
