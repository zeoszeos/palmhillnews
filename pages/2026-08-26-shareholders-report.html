Vendor Meetings & Maintenance Updates

  1. We had a car hit the fence around the reclaimed and north irrigation main pipes at the shed for golf maintenance located on Sabal. All repairs have been made.
  2. Anthony and I met with the BrightView supervisor on Aug 5, 2026. The number of issues being reported to the office has decreased since the meeting. We pointed out the areas that are being missed. He stated that he would bring in an additional crew to address ongoing issues. We recommended that he follow up on the visits the day after mowing, as it is hard to see missed items when they are still in progress. We also recommended that if the same areas are being consistently missed, the crews be rotated to different areas. We have noticed a reduction in the number of issues reported to the office since the visit, but we will continue to monitor.
  3. The freezer in the south kitchen was returned and replaced with a new one.
  4. The air conditioner leak in the north clubhouse reoccurred, and the AC company has now replaced the coil, pan, and plenum on the unit. The coils and pan were under warranty.
  5. Piper Fire was onsite and completed inspections of the fire alarm systems and hydrants in the community. One hydrant was identified as needing repair, and the issue has already been addressed. The batteries in the alarm sounder panels need to be replaced, and maintenance is addressing this. Finally, the strobe in the north library will be replaced.
  6. The south hot tub blower has been replaced again.
  7. Paint for the purple pipes was ordered, and maintenance will be getting the paint redone, as well as the pipes in the area that was struck by the car.
  8. Maintenance cleared out the brush along the south fence in the sanitation field.
  9. The primary election was held in the south clubhouse on Aug 18.
  10. Address numbers have been installed at the south clubhouse, at both ends of the building, to address delivery issues and locate the office.
  11. Grease traps were pumped out by Wind River the week of Aug 17, 2026.
  12. The pine tree in the lot at 12 Pindo East was struck by lightning. It has been removed, and the debris cleared from the area.
  13. Inliner work is anticipated to be completed next week.

Administrative Updates

  1. There was an issue with the internet at the north clubhouse. It was a coding error with Spectrum. Toni called them, and this issue was corrected, and the internet was restored.
  2. We received the check from one of the accounts that was in collections for payment in full. Attorney’s fees were collected as part of this check as well.
  3. We have put forth the Annual meeting date as Jan 20, 2027 and the budget approval date as Mar 17, 2027 for tracking and notice purposes.

Pending Board Action Items

  1. Transformer information received and is up for approval.
  2. A tax bill on one account needs to be paid to prevent losing our ability to collect on the foreclosure action.
