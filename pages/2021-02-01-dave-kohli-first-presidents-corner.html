Feb 1, 2021

Organizational Meeting Results - I am honored and pleased to advise residents of Palm Hill, that today your newly elected board of directors elected me as president. I begin this task with great enthusiasm and optimism. I also look forward to working with our new board to maintain and improve our park.

At this meeting the board also elected Pat Foor as our new Vice-President, Bob Newport as our new Treasurer, and Carolyn Story as our continuing Secretary. As your new Executive Committee, we are excited about the direction we hope to provide the park, and we are committed to an orderly, well functioning board.

Rounding out our new board are continuing members Gregg Hanke, Dick Lawler and Craig Mayer, and new members Henry Klain and Chuck Silcox. We thank our outgoing board members Carman Foti, Frank Novara, and Jack Russell for their work over the past years.

Part 2 of Organizational Meeting – After electing our Executive Committee and setting the dates for our first two full Board Meetings, the board agreed to adjourn and set a second Organizational Meeting to be held on Feb 8, 2021 at 3:00 pm.

This action was taken for two reasons. One, to allow time to recruit and reflect on potential Committee Chairpersons, and two, to add to the agenda a motion regarding how Board Meetings will be conducted.

Committee Chairpersons – I have asked each board member to provide me with an email by Feb 5, that speaks to which Committee(s) they would like to Chair. It should include a mission/vision statement and what goals and/or objectives they would be trying to achieve in 2021 in the position.

One of our goals is to tap into some of the incredible expertise and talent that many of our residents possess. We are therefore reaching out to all residents to invite them to become part of one of our committees. We do this because we want our committees to be a much bigger influence on what happens at the board level. Please contact any board member if you are interested in helping.

Also, as committee chairs do not need to be a board member, by way of this web blast, I am also inviting any Palm Hill resident who might want to chair a committee to send me an email [personal contact details removed for the web archive] expressing their interest in doing so by Feb 5. Again, it should include a mission/vision statement and what goals and/or objectives they would be trying to achieve in 2021 in the position. If more than one person desires to chair a certain committee, I will recommend my choice to the board, and we will vote on who the chair will be.

Our current committees are: Buildings/grounds, Communications, Election, Environment, Finance, Future Planning, Golf, Real Estate, and Rules/Regulations. In addition, I would like to look into the possibility of an on-line election for next year. If an appropriate chair steps forward, I would ask the board to either create another committee for this purpose, or possibly add it to the current Election Committee.

Board Meetings and Conduct of Board Members – tomorrow I will be distributing to all board members a motion which asks them to approve a set of guidelines for conducting board meetings. In addition to electing Committee Chairpersons, this motion will be an agenda item for this Feb 8, 2021 meeting.

My Perceived Role as President – I would like residents to understand why I agreed to take on this position. My intention is to concentrate on the future of the park by operating as follows:

1. Anticipating what issues need to be analysed, reviewed and fit into the agendas of future board meetings.

2. Conducting board meetings that deal with future planning and policy/procedure issues that we rely on to keep the day-to-day running of the park on track.

3. Understanding the needs and desires of residents; to create a vision for the park’s future that a good majority of the residents can support.

4. Focusing the work of our committees on the above issues, and encourage them to analyse, review and make very specific recommendations to the board.

What I ask for to be Successful in This Role – So that I might concentrate on the role described above, I am asking the following of board members and residents:

1. To understand and respect that the Vice-President and Treasurer, in concert with the Park Manager will be dealing with all day-to-day issues and advising me accordingly as necessary.

2. That residents make all complaints, or suggestions for changes to day-to-day activities and procedures to either the Vice-President or Treasurer, who will update me, and make any necessary recommendations they feel I or the board should consider.

3. My belief is that the board’s success is in part determined by the success we have at the committee level. Effective and timely work by the committees would be invaluable for the board to have the kind of success I believe we would all like it to have.

Goals and Objectives – My goals for 2021 will be very broad and encompassing as follows:

1. I plan to chair a very forward thinking and effective board of directors.

2. I hope to create effective and timely committees that complement the board.

3. I hope to foster a “we are all in this together” attitude in the park.

4. I expect to create a plan to tackle what I believe is a serious infrastructure problem.

5. I plan to put in place strategies to deal with major future issues like next year’s election, a new Reserve Study in 2022, the expiry of the Spectrum contract in 2023, the rent negotiation in 2024, and any other long term contracts.

6. I hope to understand and put in place what further amenities residents want and are prepared to pay for and/or raise funds for.

Closing – I want to assure all residents and board members that I will continue to work hard on making our park “the best it can be”. I will remain keenly interested in ensuring that our finances are well managed, and I will do my best to conduct well run and productive board meetings. Thank you for your support.

Dave Kohli, President
