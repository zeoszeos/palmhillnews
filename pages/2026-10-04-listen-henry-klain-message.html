Status Update

1. Working Together with Management

Over the past year, we have continued to engage with our management company to discuss ways we can mutually improve the services provided to Palm Hill. These conversations have focused not only on management's responsibilities, but also on how we, as a cooperative, can better support those efforts. The discussions have been positive and productive as we work together to improve day-to-day service while maintaining cost efficiency for our shareholders.

2. Strategic Planning

Strategic Planning, including consideration of the recommendations from our recent third-party review, has primarily been assigned to our Planning & Environmental Committee. Our initial focus is on opportunities that can be achieved in the short term by addressing “low-hanging fruit,” much of which involves eliminating duplication of amenities and improving operational efficiency. These are relatively low-cost initiatives that can produce meaningful benefits while positioning us to develop one amenity into a true top-tier facility.

3. Solar Initiative

Our solar initiative continues to make significant progress. The north solar array is now operational, and the largest array in the south has also been commissioned in “island mode.” As part of the final phase of the project, we are evaluating the need to upgrade electrical infrastructure, including transformer capacity, to enable us to reverse-feed electricity back to the grid. Once completed, Palm Hill will continue to realize meaningful electrical savings for many years to come. Our sincere thanks go to the many volunteers whose dedication and expertise have brought this project to the threshold of completion.

4. Understanding Who We Are

I would also like to encourage a broader discussion about something that makes Palm Hill truly unique. Palm Hill is not a condominium (Condo), nor is it a Homeowners Association (HOA). Palm Hill is a cooperative. Each of our 1,096 shareholders owns one share in the cooperative, and together we collectively own and democratically govern our community.

The word cooperative has two complementary meanings:

  • As an adjective, it describes people who are willing to work together, help one another, and contribute toward common goals.
  • As a noun (often called a “co-op”), it describes a business or organization that is owned and democratically controlled by the people who use its services.

These two definitions beautifully describe Palm Hill. We are not simply neighbors living in the same community; we are owners working together to shape its future.

As Palm Hill continues to evolve and adapt to changing times, understanding and embracing our cooperative model will become increasingly important. It encourages participation, volunteerism, shared responsibility, and thoughtful decision-making for the benefit of everyone.

I invite everyone to reflect on what it truly means to be part of a cooperative. We look forward to continuing this conversation as we discuss Palm Hill's future and the opportunities that lie ahead.

Thank you to all our committees, continuing in partnership with shareholders, doing the hard work required, in the best interest of our community.

Stay tuned.

On behalf of the Board,
Henry Klain
President, Home Owners of Palm Hill (HOPH)
