Weekly Manager Update

From the Palm Hill office · Megan Leftwich, On-Site Manager

Friday, Oct 9, 2026

Good Afternoon Everyone,

  • The office will be open on Monday. Toni and I will be here. Jess is on vacation next week.
  • There was a short in one of the smoke detectors in the south clubhouse last weekend. It set off the alarm several times, and the fire department responded. Piper Fire was out Sunday morning but couldn’t locate the source. Since the alarm system was set to be offline, we locked down the building and notified shareholders. Piper returned on Monday, replaced both smoke detectors in the lobby, and reconnected the pull station. Everything was up and running on Monday, so we reopened the clubhouse. The fire inspector stopped by on Tuesday and I let him know that they had come out, repairs had been made and everything was operational again.
  • We have ordered signs for the sanitation field and the office with hours. Maintenance mounted the one at the field on a pole by the fob reader (photo below). The one for the office will be mounted on the glass next to the front doors.
  • Jess has updated the “Returning to Palm Hill” and “Leaving Palm Hill” forms this week. We are trying to capture and update all contact information for those coming into the park for the season. It is included in the Manager’s Corner article that was submitted, along with a couple of paragraphs reminding homeowners to submit applications for work prior to commencing work, and where they can find information on what is allowed.
  • Lynntina has stated that she has completed the engineering design for the new transformer. A Duke representative was on-site on Tuesday, and we discussed the situation with him. He specializes in commercial metering and expects to be on-site when the transformer is installed. She stated the invoice would be sent out in a couple of days, but we have not yet received it. Additionally, they are looking for us to provide locates and sign a waiver for them to complete the work.
  • I’ve reached out to Spectrum to follow up on them cleaning up the coaxial lines now that we have made the switch to fiber everywhere. I received replies from them and they are looping in the appropriate people to determine where this stands.
  • A hose on the recycling compactor ruptured last Friday and they came out and made the repair. It is up and running correctly again.
  • Income taxes have been received.
  • The City of Largo has reached out to remind us that the annual manhole inspections are due in the month of Nov. We are confirming the appointment for them to be completed.

New Sanitation Field Hours sign by the fob reader: 9am–3pm Monday–Friday, 9am–noon Saturday, closed Sundays & holidays.

Sincerely,
Megan Leftwich, CMCA, AMS
On-Site Manager
