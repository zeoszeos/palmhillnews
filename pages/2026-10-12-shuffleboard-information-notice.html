With shuffleboard season approaching, just a few reminders:
  • For safety reasons, please do not walk on the courts.
  • After use, return the disc to holders, and place under benches.
  • After use, return cue sticks to wall hangers.
  • When leaving the buildings, close the windows and turn off the lights and fans.
  • All persons under 18 years old must be accompanied by an adult.
  • No smoking.
  • For safety, wear closed toe shoes
Additional information for the north courts with embedded beads:
  • Do not drop the disc directly onto the beaded surface, as that can dislodge the beads. Place the disc outside of the playing area and push them forward into the kitchen to start play.
Thank you from the Shuffleboard Club.
