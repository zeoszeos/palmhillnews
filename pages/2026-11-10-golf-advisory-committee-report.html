Date: November 10, 2:00 p.m. Report by: Randy Mitchell, for Joe Clay Next meeting: December 8, 2:00 p.m., South Boardroom

Course Conditions and Maintenance

  • Seeding of the fairways with winter ryegrass is scheduled for 11/21.
  • Blue and white markers on some tee boxes will stay in the same location to allow the grass to grow.
  • During winter play, use the cart path to allow grass to grow. The 90-degree rule is enforced.
  • Play has increased. It is important to repair divots on the greens and tee boxes.
  • Tree trimming on the fifth hole is out for bids and will be reviewed through the Buildings and Grounds Committee.

Membership Report (Megan)

  • 126 paid memberships
  • 67 paid golf cart fees
  • 14 10-packs sold

Fiscal Year Transition (Megan)

Megan presented and explained the process and transition timeline for changing the golf committee fiscal year. A partial prorated season would align future billing with the new fiscal calendar.

Decision: The committee voted to table this for now, to allow more understanding and information on the impact on membership.

Board Meeting Summary

Carolyn Story presented to the board the summary of the last golf meeting, including plans for the upcoming Taste of Golf, a valuable opportunity for all shareholders to experience our beautiful golf course.

Association and League Reports

Women's Association (Sue Waldecker)

  • There are now 24 players and one new member.
  • The Ladies Golf Association meeting is on 11/13.
  • A new app, Golf League Tracker, is being used to calculate handicaps, prepare pairings and print scorecards.

Men's Association (Lawrence Lotz)

  • The annual dues payment process was streamlined this year: members complete information on an envelope, insert annual dues and turn it in at the pro shop.
  • Members are reminded to sign in before each round of golf.
  • Pro shop staff will be on site 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Lawrence suggested a possible volunteer sheet for members to staff the pro shop after the staff leave.

Couples League (Mike McCarthy)

  • Everything is going along fine. There were 56 golfers last Thursday.
  • They are looking for people to host future couples golf days.

Bandits (Lawrence Lotz)

  • The season has begun and more Bandits are arriving each week.

Individual Reports

  • Landscaping (Mike McCarthy): The Men's Association meeting was looking for suggestions for course improvements and/or needs. Contact Joe Clay, Jeff Wesso or Randy Mitchell.
  • Future Planning (Mike Leahy): The goal this year is to work with Joe Clay to identify the square footage needed for equipment, and its location and placement.
  • Pro Shop (Sue Waldecker): The inside and outside were cleaned, and the new plantings look very attractive.
  • Accountability (Caroline Zimmerman): Lawrence Lotz will be working on the volunteer list to assist pro shop staff after hours.

Taste of Golf (Carolyn Zimmerman)

  • Date: November 18.
  • 82 golfers signed up to date.
  • Groupings are complete for the 13 stations, with 40 volunteers.
  • The golf course will be closed that day from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Next Meeting

December 8, 2:00 p.m., South Boardroom.
