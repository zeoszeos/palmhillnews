A Message from Board President Henry Klain

Announcement: Land Lease Reduction and Budget Update

Wednesday, Jun 4, 2025🔊 Listen →
President Message Archives → · Newsletter Archive →

We are pleased to announce that a significantly reduced land lease valuation of $48,000,000 has been successfully achieved.

On behalf of the Board of Directors, we want to assure shareholders that we are fully committed to passing on this financial benefit by reducing monthly maintenance fees as soon as possible.

To that end, the Finance Committee has begun preliminary work to revise the 2025–2026 annual budget. A special meeting of the Committee will be held on Monday, Jun 9, at 2:00 p.m. to present a proposed revised budget. Following that, the revised budget will require Board approval to initiate the mandatory 30-day notification and mail-out period, after which a second Board meeting will be held to review and vote on final approval.

We will continue to provide updates as this process moves forward with urgency and care.

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc
