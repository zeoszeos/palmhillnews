I understand there have been questions regarding the status of Palm Hill’s land purchase initiative. This matter is presently being addressed and deserves further consideration.

The potential purchase has been discussed at the P&E Committee, where the view was expressed that the initiative is financially viable and warrants further exploration by the Board. On September 30th, the Board accepted that advice and unanimously adopted the following motion to establish a Land Advisory Team:

Motion to Establish a Land Advisory Team

I move that the Board of Directors establish a “Land Advisory Team” to research and evaluate Palm Hill’s potential purchase of the land currently leased from John S. Taylor Properties LLC and to report its findings and recommendations to the Board of Directors.

The Land Advisory Team will be responsible for gathering and evaluating information related to:

  • Potential purchase price and associated costs;
  • Available financing and loan options;
  • The potential impact of a land purchase on shareholder maintenance fees;
  • Alternative payment and financing structures;
  • Relevant surveys and shareholder feedback regarding the potential purchase, including the required supporting vote of two-thirds of shareholders; and
  • Other information the team determines necessary to assist the Board in evaluating the matter.

I further move that the following individuals be appointed to the Land Advisory Team:

  • Dave Kohli
  • Craig Mayer
  • Greg Lockwood — Board Liaison

The team may request additional members, professional expertise, or outside assistance as necessary to complete its work, subject to Board approval where appropriate.

The Land Advisory Team will serve in an advisory capacity only. It will report its findings to the Board of Directors, and any decision regarding the purchase of the leased land will remain with the Board and, where required, the shareholders.

With the advisory team now established, our next step is to advance the work necessary to determine whether we can secure the required two-thirds shareholder support for proceeding with this initiative. We need to understand the financial implications, available options, and approval requirements so that shareholders can make an informed decision.

In my opinion, this is one of the highest priorities we collectively face today. If the purchase proves achievable on reasonable terms, it could provide an immediate financial benefit and greater long-term stability for the portion of our maintenance fees associated with our land lease obligations.

We owe it to ourselves, as shareholders and as directors entrusted with the future of Palm Hill, to give this opportunity the time and serious consideration it deserves. That means examining the facts, understanding the risks and alternatives, and ensuring that shareholders have the information necessary to make an informed decision.

We cannot afford to dismiss an opportunity of this magnitude based on short-term thinking or comfortable assumptions about the status quo. Our responsibility is to look beyond today and consider what will best serve Palm Hill over the long term.

I expect that town halls, further communications and transparency will come forth shortly, as we all organize our thoughts and intentions.

If we want to preserve an affordable lifestyle, maintain a safe community, and protect the many things Palm Hill has successfully accomplished, we must be prepared to examine opportunities that could strengthen our financial future.

Let us give this initiative the attention it deserves and exercise our fiduciary responsibilities with the long-term interests of Palm Hill and its shareholders firmly in mind.

Respectfully,
Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc
