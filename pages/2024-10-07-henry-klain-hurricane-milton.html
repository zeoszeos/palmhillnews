President’s Message - Hurricane Milton

Dear fellow Palm Hill owners and residents:

Unfortunately, we have in the past and will continue to experience tropical storms that have the ability to threaten lives and cause significant property damage. The present weather situation is that we have not completely recovered from Hurricane Helene and are now having to prepare to face Hurricane Milton, which may become the biggest storm this area has faced in our lifetime.

Our Resource Property Management staff, with the help and cooperation of many residents, have worked diligently in the clean up of debris caused by Hurricane Helene. The maintenance team continues to work hard to ensure as much as possible of the debris can be cleared from the streets of Palm Hill. Although Largo recently emptied our vegetation bin, their pick-up ability is strained to give us the bin capacity to finish the job.

We recognize those of you who have experienced damage to their units and, although repairs may not have been addressed due to the recency of the last storm, it is expected and appreciated that, at minimum, homes are as secured as possible from the recent storm damage.

A State of Emergency has been declared by the State of Florida, and Pinellas County has issued voluntary evacuations for mobile home parks. It is highly probable that this will be upgraded to a mandatory evacuation order by later today. Please ensure you have secured your units and have plans to evacuate to safe areas such as official hurricane shelters. To those of you in Palm Hill at the moment, please check on your neighbors, particularly those living alone, and ensure they are aware of the seriousness of the impending storm and can be safely accommodated.

Over the next day or two, please pay attention to emergency alerts. As required, our management team will also issue messaging as, in solidarity, we move through and past this major weather event.

Your Board members, along with myself and Megan, are in regular communication and maintaining focus on emergency preparedness and, most importantly, everyone’s safety.

Safety and protection of life remains paramount! Physical assets can be replaced.

Florida Strong!

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc.

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email from Resource Property Management on behalf of Home Owners of Palm Hill, Inc on Monday, October 7, 2024, at 11:42 AM ET.
