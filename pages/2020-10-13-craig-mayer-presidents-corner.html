President’s Corner - October 13, 2020

Regrettably, once again we find ourselves having to suffer Board member Carmen Foti’s misuse of emails and his misrepresentations in them. I am referring to his email rant of 11 October if you were unfortunate enough to have received it. As Chairman of the Board of Directors I apologize that I can do little more than extend my apologies for his behavior and intrusion into resident’s lives via email, as well as his repeated sending of residents’ private email addresses out to hundreds of people they may not know. His emails are not approved or sanctioned in any way by the Board of Directors.

Getting straight to the point, Mr. Foti’s claims about the proposed rule changes are as preposterous and confused as they are deliberately intended to sow fear and mislead residents. The proposed rule changes no existing rule and it only deals with the use of the Parks streets or parking lots. It is intended to ensure that the Park office gets adequate advance notice of any planned parades, block parties, or other celebrations that would impede traffic flow, block streets, or potentially affect resident safety. Please read the rule proposal memorandum that was sent to all board members on October 9th. It is attached to this message and explains the reason for the rule. Worth noting is that Mr. Foti’s recital of the rule’s text omits the fact (and the words) that it deals only with the use of the Park’s streets or parking lots. It is a traffic control and safety measure.

Not to be outdone in the realm of misinformation and distortion is Mr. Foti’s second set of claims – those related to rec club lawsuits and the control of clubs. Kindly put, Mr. Foti’s claims are pure fiction. The best I can figure is that he is referring to the Covid-19 Park Manager Closure Order and Lawsuit Settlement Agreement Violation Notice that was sent to the South Rec Club President last April by Association Legal Counsel. The letter addresses Mr. Foti’s reckless and irresponsible behavior in violating the Park Manager’s Covid-19 Closure Order and the South Rec Club’s continuing responsibility to comply with the Agreement. A copy of that letter is attached. It speaks for itself and is worth a careful reading.

Finally, on another topic, please note that at our Board meeting on Wednesday we will be discussing in a workshop setting updates to the Covid-19 protocols and policies that have been issued and those that may be issued. Board members are now reviewing the Park Manager’s proposed course of action regarding the reopening of the north clubhouse as well as both shuffleboard courts. As Palm Hill’s fall and winter seasons commence the Board is looking to have a set of protocols in place that allow for residents to enjoy amenities and activities while continuing to guard against the spread of Covid-19 in the Park.

Please remember, public health authorities continue to emphasize that all wear masks everywhere, wash your hands frequently, stay at least six (6) feet apart, and avoid social gatherings of any kind - particularly those that are indoors whether at Palm Hill or elsewhere.

Thank you for supporting Palm Hill,

Craig Mayer
President
HOPH, Inc.
