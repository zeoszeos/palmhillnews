Good Afternoon Everyone,

  • The system with work orders through Vantaca being sent to BrightView seems to be working well. An owner reported an issue on Tuesday, which was put through the system. The owner responded later in the day, saying they had returned to address the issue. We have also had a couple of others reported, and the vendor is responding to emails confirming that the work has been completed.
  • Here's a helpful article that gives a clear, easy refresher on what fiduciary duty means. It's a quick read and worth a moment of your time. While the article references 720 and 718, the section that applies to Palm Hill is 719.1035(9)(a), which states: "The officers and directors of the association have a fiduciary relationship to the unit owners." https://www.campbellpropertymanagement.com/blog/board-member-fiduciary-duties-owed-to-the-association
  • The invoice for the transformer was paid with a check yesterday. Once they have confirmed receipt, the transformer installation will take approximately 6 weeks.
  • The liner for the sewer along Fern was ordered last Friday. It is expected to arrive next Friday, so we anticipate the installation should occur during the week of 10/26. The information on the road repair is still being discussed.
  • We have started using the new reservation system for the Board room and the smaller conference room. It seems easy to use, and I have been keeping it updated as meetings are scheduled. Jess will remain the primary person responsible for checking and scheduling events in those two rooms.
  • The outside kitchen doors were replaced on Tuesday. Maintenance has been assigned to paint the new ones.
  • The hole in the wall where the water fountain was outside the office has been closed up. The wall has been painted, but still needs another coat. The baseboards will be reinstalled once the painting is completed.
  • The window repairs from the tree struck by lightning is scheduled to be completed on Tuesday.
  • I am ordering more green clay for the bocce courts. The clay that was installed after the reconstruction has settled and it is in need of a refresh.
  • New sign with office hours was installed at the south clubhouse near the fob reader. Picture attached.

Sincerely,
