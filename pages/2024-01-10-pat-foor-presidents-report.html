President’s Report

On Jan 8, 2024, a camera was installed in the South Recreation Hall Lobby without prior notification or board authority. This camera has been removed. There are two functioning video cameras in that lobby. Any shareholder may request a copy of the video from Park Manager, Megan DiBello, in writing, and provide an empty thumb drive to copy the requested time frame.

The process for recording the proxies has been consistent for years. Due to recent allegations of impropriety, going forward, the ballot box will now be located inside the office window. When you bring your ballot and proxy to the window, your ballot will be noted on a spreadsheet and then placed in the box. You may wait to observe this being done. The proxy will also be logged to ensure quorum for the election on Jan 17, 2024, but will not go into the ballot box. Ballots will not be removed from the ballot box until Jan 17th.

Any election complaints may be filed after the election occurs.

Thank you,

Pat Foor, President
Home Owners of Palm Hill, Inc.

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email on Wednesday, Jan 10, 2024, at 4:23 PM ET.
