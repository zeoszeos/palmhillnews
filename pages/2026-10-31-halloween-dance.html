Update from Linda Keehl, Oct 8

The Halloween Dance is at the North clubhouse. The doors will open for you at 6:15 and there is no assigned seating ahead of time.

The tickets are sold out as of now but stay tuned as there are some tickets that may become available if not picked up and paid for.

Don't forget a treat to share for our table- to -table trick or treating and the costume contest. Hope you will come and enjoy the Haunted Library- cost is $2 or free with your dance ticket. More details are on the bulletin boards in the clubhouses.

Source: Palm Hill Webmaster notice from Linda Keehl, Oct 8
