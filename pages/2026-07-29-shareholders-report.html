Vendor Meetings & Maintenance Updates

  1. Pye Barker has completed repairs on the Ansul system in the north clubhouse kitchen after some scheduling issues.
  2. Annual fire alarm and hydrant inspections have been scheduled with Piper Fire for Aug 5, 2026. They will be on-site at 7 am and begin with the fire hydrants. A notice will be sent to homeowners on Aug 4 to notify them of this inspection.
  3. United AC has been working on the north ice machine. They have determined that it needs a new water inlet valve. Additionally, a new wicking kit has been ordered for the refrigerator in the south kitchen. They will be back out to complete these repairs and address the broken door handle in the north.
  4. The interconnect application for solar panels for the north was approved by Duke, and the system was switched “on” on Jul 6. We are still waiting on the completion of the plan for the south transformer. Once that occurs, there will be a cost to present to the board for the upgrade. Until this occurs, the interconnect application will not be approved.
  5. The monthly inspection of the lift station identified that it needed new Contactors and Overloads. These were approved and replaced. They also cleared the pumps of rags and debris. A reminder to all residents: please only put disposable items down the drains, such as food through the garbage disposal or toilet paper, as anything that doesn’t disintegrate causes clogs and requires expensive repairs.
  6. The sun canopy in the south pool area has been completed. It was initially installed off-center, and the contractor returned to address the issue. The permit inspections have occurred, and the permit has been cleared by the City of Largo.
  7. The north pool area was closed on Jul 15, and maintenance made repairs to the cracks in the pool deck. Anthony has advised that the stucco should be considered for updating due to the appearance of these repairs and previous repairs.
  8. Maintenance repaired the shut-off valve at the south irrigation pump area. The south well is now up and running.
  9. FDOT was onsite to inspect the culvert that runs underneath Seminole Boulevard from Palm Hill’s south pond.
  10. The permit for the generator at the lift station was finally cleared by the City of Largo, and this project is now completed. To date, we have paid $18,058 for the generator, which is 50% of the installation cost and the gas line installation cost. We are expecting a bill from the contractor for the remaining balance of $13,900.

Administrative Updates

  1. The telephones that are utilized by maintenance personnel have been updated to include the newest employee. Additionally, we were able to eliminate two phone numbers from the account. The phone used in the Pro-shop had the battery burst; a claim was filed, and it was replaced.
  2. Jess has been diligently working to digitize older records so that we no longer have to keep the paper versions. This eliminates the need for space to store them and/or for paying to store them offsite.
  3. We received the check from one of the accounts that was in collections for payment in full. Attorney’s fees were collected as part of this check as well.

Pending Board Action Items

  1. Approve bid for replacement of North AC unit.
  2. Transformer will be up for approval once quote is received from engineer.
