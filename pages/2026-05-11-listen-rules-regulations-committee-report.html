Rules and Regulation Committee

May 11, 2026 1 pm

Boardroom, Zoom, and Ch.732

The Rules and Regulations Meeting was called to order at 1pm, and adjourned at 2:30pm. Although the length of the meeting was fairly long the number of Topics was limited. Discussion of a Firearms rule was briefly discussed but was tabled until further input could be accessed. There was also a brief discussion on Speed bumps but was tabled for future discussion. There was a lot of discussion on ESA dogs, but it is to be further reviewed in a Future meeting. The Committee reviewed the proposed Violations Policy, and made adjustments to it. It was then approved to be sent to the Board for adoption. The next meeting was set for August 10, 2026, at 1pm. Then the meeting was adjourned

Submitted by Bob Newport, Committee Laison
