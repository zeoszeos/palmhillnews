Palm Hill history, a little at a time

From your Palm Hill Communications team

Forty years ago this month, on November 7, 1986, the State declared Home Owners of Palm Hill, Inc. officially a cooperative. That makes 2026 our 40th year owning our own park! So each month we’ll dig a little into our past, from Candy Armstrong Schmidt’s 1987 Story of Palm Hill Country Club and from the back issues of the LINK. See how you do!

Did you know?

• Our golf course opened to the public in late November 1970. You played five holes, walked west through the citrus grove with the mockingbirds singing, then played the last holes.

• In 1970 the only phone in the park was in the sales office, and everyone’s mail went there too.

• Snowbirds have always gotten a party. On November 4, 1979, “the season” opened at North Hall with champagne punch, coffee and cake... all free!

10 years ago in the LINK (November 2016): the North Recreation Club’s Veterans Day program on November 11th at the North Clubhouse, with free coffee and donuts at 8:00, the program at 9:00 and a potluck at noon. And a reminder to snowbirds to check in with the office so your fob works at the pools and clubhouses. Some things never change! :)
