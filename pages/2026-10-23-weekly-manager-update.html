  • David had asked if the insurance coverage was necessary and Lauren responded with the following:

    The coverage is not mandatory, but a recommendation considering the prior policy included the coverage.

    Below are examples of claim scenarios that would preclude coverage without implementing such a policy:

    Scenario 1 – Neighbor Dispute Turns Physical
    Two residents get into a heated argument over a parking space in a common lot. The dispute escalates and one resident strikes the other. The injured party sues the park, alleging negligent security and failure to address prior complaints about the aggressive neighbor.

    Scenario 2 – Pool / Common Area Altercation
    A fight breaks out between teenagers at the community pool. A bystander resident is injured trying to break it up. The park is named in the lawsuit for failing to provide adequate supervision or security.

    Scenario 3 – On-Site Manager Involvement
    A park manager physically confronts a resident who is violating community rules. The resident alleges excessive force and files a claim against both the manager and the park ownership entity.
