Call the Palm Hill office at (727) 581-8729 to arrange disposal.

Two trailers are available: one for trash and one for recyclables. Place items on the correct trailer. Break down and tie cardboard so it cannot blow away. Compactors are unavailable for safety reasons.

Recycling Guide

Recycle These

  • Plastic containers with a recycling symbol
  • Mixed paper, newspapers, and magazines
  • Aluminum cans, foil, and trays
  • Tin and steel food cans
  • Clear, green, or brown glass
  • Cardboard and pizza boxes
  • Milk and juice paper cartons

Do Not Recycle

  • Styrofoam and take-out containers
  • Plastic bags and plastic wrap
  • Yard waste or construction material
  • Food scraps
  • Soiled paper towels or toilet tissue
  • Dryer sheets or lint
  • Clothing or shoes
  • Pet waste
  • Latex gloves or feminine-hygiene products
