Our Community

Palm Hill Country Club is a cooperative manufactured-home community. Its homeowners share ownership through Home Owners of Palm Hill, Inc., which maintains community streets, major water and sewer lines, clubhouses, recreation areas, and other common facilities.

The community includes 1,096 homes and residents from across the United States, Canada, and overseas.

Facilities & Recreation

  • Nine-hole, par-30 golf course and Pro Shop
  • Two clubhouses and recreation clubs
  • Swimming pools and Jacuzzis
  • Three tennis courts
  • Pickleball and horseshoes
  • Two bocce courts
  • Covered and open-air shuffleboard courts
  • Two billiards rooms
  • Two libraries
  • Laundry facilities

Community Life

Resident activities include clubs, cards, crafts, travel, dinners, dances, sports, and other social programs. Regular board meetings and workshops give homeowners opportunities to stay informed and participate in community governance.

Central Largo Location

Palm Hill is located at 1800 Seminole Boulevard in Largo, close to the Pinellas Trail, Gulf beaches, shopping, airports, parks, and Tampa Bay attractions.

For current fees, rules, availability, and official community information, use the Palm Hill website or contact the office at (727) 581-8729.
