🏘️ Palm Hill Shareholders Report

Administrative Updates
1. The computer that Anthony uses for maintenance has been added to monitoring to prevent viruses or other issues.
2. Golf Cart stickers and 10-pack cards were received. An email notifying residents that memberships are available for purchase for the 2026-2027 year was sent out.
3. We received the check from the sale of 837 Cane Palm. Rules & Regulations and a welcome letter for Vantaca access were provided to the trustee.
4. Wind River has updated their contact person. Communication is ongoing to ensure all regular maintenance/inspections are properly scheduled.
5. RPM has been in touch with the CPA to ensure taxes are being completed timely.
Vendor Meetings & Maintenance Updates
6. Accurate Electronics repaired the exit button at the south pool and corrected the front door wiring to follow the schedule.
7. Transform Solar completed solar panel installation on the south clubhouse. Interconnect applications submitted to Duke Energy (technical review phase in progress).
8. Termites were found in the south kitchen area. Terminex inspected and treated the area (warranty for the south building in place).
9. Storm drain grates were inspected community-wide. One replaced; two more on order.
10. The Variable Frequency Drive has been installed by Citrus and the south well is now up and running.
11. Maintenance cleaned up the fenced area outside the south clubhouse. A message was sent by the Bazaar director regarding items left in the area.
12. A new key box was installed in the office.
13. Wind screens have been installed at the tennis courts.
14. The sun canopy for the south clubhouse is in the permitting stage (Suncoast Renovations working with City of Largo).
15. The IFB for the north clubhouse air conditioner was released to vendors on 6/17. Pre-bid meeting scheduled; bids due for the B&G meeting on 7/8.
