📊 Monthly Shareholders Report
Monthly Management and Maintenance Report
July 15, 2026
VENDOR MEETINGS & MAINTENANCE UPDATES
1. Accurate Electronics was onsite to complete a repair to the exit button at the south pool. Additionally, they corrected the wiring on the front door so that it correctly follows the schedule.
2. Transform Solar has completed the installation of the solar panels on the south clubhouse. Interconnect applications with Duke Energy are submitted and are currently in the technical review phase. Once the technical review is complete we expect to receive the quote to replace the transformer.
3. The south kitchen area had evidence of termites. Terminex was out to inspect and treat the area. They provide the warranty for the south building.
4. Storm drain grates throughout the community have been inspected. We have replaced one that was rusting and have an additional two on order. Once they are received maintenance will get them installed.
5. The Variable Frequency Drive has been installed by Citrus and the south well is up and running.
6. Maintenance cleaned up the fenced area outside of the south clubhouse. A message was sent out by the Bazaar director to homeowners not to leave items in this area.
7. A new key box was installed in the office. We have updated and added keys and outgrew the previous one.
8. Wind screens have been installed at the tennis courts.
9. The sun canopy for the south clubhouse is in the permitting stage and they have had some back and forth between Suncoast Renovations and the City of Largo. Once the permit is granted, the work should be completed quickly.
10. The IFB for the north clubhouse air conditioner was released to the vendors on 6/17. There is an upcoming pre-bid meeting to go over all of the details with the vendors and the bids will be due for the B&G meeting scheduled for 7/8. 7 of the 8 companies that received the bid information have responded and are expected to be in attendance.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATES
1. The computer that Anthony uses for maintenance has been added to the monitoring to prevent viruses or other issues.
2. Golf Cart stickers and 10-pack cards were received. An email notifying residents that memberships are available for purchase for the 2026-2027 year was sent out.
3. We received the check from the sale of 837 Cane Palm. I have reached out to the trustee and provided them with Rules & Regulations as well as a welcome letter for access to Vantaca.
4. Wind River has updated their contact person. I have been in communication with the new person to ensure that all regular maintenance/inspections provided by them are on their calendar to be completed at the correct time moving forward.
5. RPM has been in touch with the CPA to ensure that the taxes are being completed timely.
