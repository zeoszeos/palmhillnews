President’s Corner

Mar, 2022

We’re enjoying beautiful days as residents celebrate being together with block parties, musical activities, sporting and social events before our snowbirds head north for the summer. Spring break has brought visiting guests into our park to spend time with family and friends. Be sure to get dashboard permits for visitors’ vehicles and have your guests wear visitors’ badges to the pools and indoor activities.

Cautiously enter intersections in vehicles, including golf carts and bicycles, after fully stopping to check for incoming traffic. We know of at least one accident and some close calls recently so always carefully move around the park maintaining the 15 miles per hour speed limit.

I was asked if the pool incident offenders were identified. Yes they were and have been dealt with per our Rules and Regulations.

The Park Manager, Rich, has arranged flea spraying for the common areas as soon as the contractor confirms a mid-Apr date.

Our toilet repair program seems to be having an impact on water bill savings. Many factors impact the park’s water bill. If each of us does what we can to conserve water usage we can continue to have a positive impact on future bills. Volunteers are now repairing the leaks once identified. If your toilet(s) still needs to be checked get your test tablets from the office to report any additional leaks.

Volunteers make Palm Hill the desirable location that it is, from serving on boards and committees to participating in activities and special projects. Recently, a volunteer group worked one Saturday to clean up the landscaping around the Golf Pro Shop. Look for their picture on the cover of the Apr LINK. That’s just one example of residents stepping up when they see a need. We thank all who contribute in whatever way they can to get involved. Pictures of activities can be sent to Dan Hannafin for posting on our website.

Some committees, such as Golf, Building and Grounds and Planning are starting hybrid meetings, combining in-person with Zoom and Channel 732 connection. When an agenda is published you will see the available format for the upcoming committee meeting.

Currently, these in-person committee meetings are being held in the South Clubhouse Board Room, with committee members participating in person or on Zoom, also seen on Channel 732. Following these meetings the Zoom replay is available on the Palm Hill website until the meeting minutes are approved and posted to replace the Zoom replay.

Watch for Web blast reminders and be sure your email is current with Debra at the office to receive these frequent messages of importance. This is the quickest, most effective way to inform residents in a timely manner.

Thank you,

Pat Foor
Board of Directors’ President
