BOARD of DIRECTORS
Henry Klain, President of HOPH, INC., Board of Directors

BOARD OF DIRECTORS RESOLVES LAND LEASE AND APPROVES REVISED BUDGET FOR MAILING

The Board of Directors has successfully resolved the 15-year land lease renewal process with the Taylor Family, owners of the Palm Hill property.

The initial appraisal process began last fall with a preliminary valuation of approximately $68 million for budgeting purposes. As required by the lease terms, three independent land appraisers were engaged, resulting in a Jun 2025 valuation of $48 million. Following additional discussions with the Taylor Family, the final agreed-upon amount was reduced to $45 million, effective Jul 1, 2025. The Taylor family graciously acknowledged their valued relationship with Palm Hill over the years.

In response to this outcome, the Finance Committee developed a revised budget. On Jun 12, the Board of Directors granted approval, for mail out to shareholders, a proposed budget that will reduce the monthly shareholder fee from $677 to $554 starting Aug. 1, 2025, a savings of $123 per month per shareholder.

A detailed explanation of the revised budget has been distributed to all shareholders by postal mail and by email to those subscribed to Palm Hill’s electronic notifications.

A final vote on the revised budget is scheduled for Jul 17, the approved budget remaining in effect through Mar 31, 2026. This revised budget incorporates the updated land lease terms effective Jul 1and includes adjustments to other Palm Hill operating expenses for Fiscal Year 2025–2026.

As part of this budget, the Board has designated $50 of each shareholder’s monthly fee to be allocated directly to the Reserve Fund, which supports ongoing maintenance of Palm Hill’s infrastructure. This contribution will add approximately $54,800 per month or $438,400 by fiscal year-end, to the Reserve Fund, which currently holds about $900,000.

Finally, shareholders are reminded that the Finance Committee will begin preparing the next annual budget this fall. As in recent years, the process will remain fully transparent, with open committee meetings and regular communications to all shareholders as the Fiscal Year 2026–2027 budget is developed.

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email on Friday, Jun 20, 2025, at 10:10 AM ET.
