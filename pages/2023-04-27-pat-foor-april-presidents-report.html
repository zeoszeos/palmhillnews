April President’s Corner

Several large projects are in the planning and implementation stages in Palm Hill and there is often crossover between committees working on the same projects. Here is a partial list of projects with project name/committee responsible/responsible person coordinating:

North Well/Planning Committee/Bill Prior
Spectrum Contract/Planning Committee/ Henry Klain
Land Lease/Executive Committee/Bill Prior
RV Expansion/Planning Committee transitioned to Buildings & Grounds Committee/ Bill Dresch and Bob Newport
Solar/Planning Committee/Henry Klain and Bob Newport
Water Leakages and Costs/Buildings & Grounds Committee/Chuck Silcox

Please direct your input to the person and/or committee working with the project as we move these initiatives forward.

We appreciate the work and input of all contributors to maintain and improve Palm Hill. We have just come through a very busy season with many additional guests in the park and now through the summer months we can get some of the needed repairs and projects accomplished.

When you become aware of misuse or abuse of our property or rule infractions please get pictures and details of the incident or infraction and submit those to our Park Manager for investigation and next steps. In an emergency situation contact me or another board member with your information/verification for follow-up. We must be very cautious to have all the facts in order to pursue next steps with violators. There are sometimes extenuating circumstances that may or may not be able to be shared.

We all have a vested interest in keeping Palm Hill the park we enjoy. Let’s each do our part to follow the rules and respect each other.

Pat Foor, President
Homeowners of Palm Hill, Inc.
