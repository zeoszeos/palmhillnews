President’s Corner

Dec 2022

I hope you are enjoying each beautiful day in Palm Hill this holiday season and making the most of time with family and friends. Board members have recently been asked why we hold closed executive sessions on occasion that are not open to shareholders. For certain topics this is permitted by Florida Statute. The following last sentence in Florida Statute 719.106 (1) (c) is the source for this exception:

“Notwithstanding any other law to the contrary, the requirement that board meetings and committee meetings be open to the unit owners does not apply to board or committee meetings held for the purpose of discussing personnel matters or meetings between a board or committee and the association’s attorney, with respect to proposed or pending litigation, if the meeting is held for the purpose of seeking or rendering legal advice.”

The entire Statute may be read at:

http://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0719/0719.html

Please follow park requests that are being publicized regarding the sewer re-lining project in the south section. Daily water shut-offs will be a frequent occurrence until the project is completed. Watch for notices and comply with requests to avoid further delay with the project completion.

The north side water over-usage investigation continues. Stay tuned for updates as we seek the source of this serious drain on our park finances. Do your part to assure you do not have leaking toilets or other leakages that may be contributing factors.

Working together, every shareholder plays a vital role in keeping our community going!

Pat Foor, President
Homeowners Association of Palm Hill, Inc.
