III. ASSESSMENTS DUE TO THE CORPORATION

A. A $50.00 transfer fee and a $50.00 administrative fee for a total of $100.00 is required to transfer records, issue documents, etc., when a home/unit is transferred. The fee shall be paid by the buyer at the time of transfer.

B. Maintenance fees and payments on notes are due on the first day of the month. Payment not received by the tenth day of the month will be considered delinquent. The Association may charge an administrative late fee (in addition to interest at the highest rate allowable by law) for any delinquent maintenance fee payment. Such fee shall not exceed the greater of twenty-five dollars ($25.00) or five percent (5%) of each delinquent maintenance fee.

Dave Bates, Board VP
