This is a friendly reminder of important pool safety regulations as outlined in our community Rules & Regulations and Florida Department of Health requirements:
Food and Beverages: All food and beverages must be kept at least 4 feet away from the edge of the pool. This 4-foot perimeter is considered the "wet deck" area and must remain clear of all food and drinks to maintain proper pool sanitation.
Glass Containers: No glass containers of any kind are permitted inside the fenced pool area. This includes bottles, jars, and glassware. Please use plastic or other non-breakable containers.
Guest Responsibility: Residents are responsible for ensuring that their guests comply with all pool rules and regulations.
These requirements help ensure the safety and enjoyment of our pool facilities for all residents. Designated seating areas beyond the wet deck provide ample space for enjoying refreshments while complying with these regulations.
We appreciate your cooperation in following these important health and safety guidelines. If you have any questions, please contact the office at (727) 581-8729.
