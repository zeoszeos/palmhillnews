A Message from Board President Henry Klain

Pleased to Share Some Positive News Regarding Land Lease

Friday, Jun 6, 2025🔊 Listen →
President Message Archives → · All Archives →

We’re very pleased to share some positive news with you.

Following further discussions with the Taylor family, we have reached an agreement on a final land value of $45,000,000for Palm Hill. This is a $3,000,000 reduction from the original appraised value and represents a generous gesture of goodwill on their part. It also reflects their appreciation of the long-standing relationship with our community and their support for a strong future for Palm Hill.

This outcome is a win for all of us. It helps ensure greater financial stability and allows us to move forward with confidence.

As a result of this agreement, the Finance Committee is now working on revising the 2025–2026 annual budget to reflect the lower land value. A special meeting will be held on Monday, Jun 9 at 2:00 p.m., where a proposed revised budget will be presented.

After that, the revised budget will go to the Board for review and preliminary approval. This step is required before we begin the official 30-day notice and mail-out period to all residents. A second Board meeting will follow for final approval.

Our goal is to implement the reduced monthly maintenance fee starting Aug 1, 2025.

We’ll continue to keep you informed every step of the way. Thank you for your continued support and engagement in our community.

Have a great weekend.

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc
