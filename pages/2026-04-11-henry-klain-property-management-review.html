Community Update on Property Management Review

Fellow Shareholders,

Following the Property Management Research Team’s completion of its information gathering and analysis, and the subsequent town hall presentation in December, the Board of Directors (BOD) determined it was important to obtain direct feedback from the community regarding both the findings and the potential path forward for Palm Hill.

This outreach generated more than 200 responses, reflecting a broad range of perspectives—from concerns to positive feedback, and everything in between. Upon review, several key themes emerged.

There was a clear preference for maintaining a full-service management model, with limited support for either self-management or a hybrid approach. Additionally, the number of homeowners advocating for a change in property management companies was approximately equal to those who preferred to retain our current provider, subject to meaningful improvements. The Board itself found its views similarly divided.

In light of this, and in recognition of the long-standing relationship with our current management company, the BOD elected to focus on strengthening and improving the existing partnership with Resource Property Management (RPM), contingent upon addressing the concerns that led to the original research initiative.

As a next step, members of the Board developed a comprehensive deficiencies report identifying 30 specific items requiring attention. This report was formally submitted to RPM on February 6, 2026.

An in-person meeting between BOD Officers and RPM leadership was held on February 23, 2026, to review these concerns. RPM subsequently provided a detailed corrective action matrix on March 4, 2026, outlining each issue along with proposed solutions. These actions include improvements to response times, process enhancements, reassignment of certain responsibilities, additional training, and staffing adjustments.

A follow-up in-person meeting was held on March 31, 2026, to assess progress. Several items have been resolved, while others remain in progress. This is an ongoing effort that will continue to be closely monitored in the coming months. RPM has expressed a strong commitment to delivering meaningful improvements, and the BOD will continue working collaboratively with them to achieve the desired outcomes for Palm Hill.

A further evaluation, including consideration of alternative management options if warranted, will take place later this year.

If you would like additional information, please feel free to contact me directly, or by board e-mail at HOPHBoardPalmHill@gmail.com

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc
