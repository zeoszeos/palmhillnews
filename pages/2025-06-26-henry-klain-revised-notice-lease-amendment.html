Dear Shareholders and Residents, further to our Palm Hill Lease, I am pleased to confirm that, following unanimous Board approval on June 25, 2025, I have today, June 26, 2025, signed the Second Amendment to our land lease agreement with the Taylors.

This amendment contributes to Palm Hill’s financial stability for the next 15 years, and positions us well for the future. The amended lease will come into effect on July 1, 2025. A copy will be available shortly on Vantaca.

On behalf of the Board and community, I would like to express our sincere appreciation to our legal counsel at Adams & Reese. Their continued professionalism and sound legal advice remain a benchmark in the industry.

Have a great day.

Henry Klain
President, HOPH Inc
