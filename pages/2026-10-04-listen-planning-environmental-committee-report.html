Planning and Environment 9/9/26 meeting summary submitted by Elaine Cole, chair.

The P&E committee heard that Buildings and Grounds is continuing to work on providing a quiet area for exercise classes B&G is also getting prices on removable window coverings to ensure classes are private.

P&E tabled the laundry service discussion of vendors because not all vendors had responded yet.

All interested vendors offer a revenue share with PH.

P&E members heard David Kohli’s presentation of the land purchase feasibility team’s conclusions.

Dave discussed the high rate of positive responses from our shareholders to the team’s survey on affordability.

He also answered questions about availability of funding.

The committee was satisfied that the team had met its burden of establishing that a purchase offer to buy the land that HOPH, Inc. currently rents, is feasible.

Having found that owning the land is advantageous for future planning, and that a purchase is feasible, the committee moved to send the report to the Board for it to take appropriate next steps.
