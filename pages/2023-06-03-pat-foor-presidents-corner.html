June, 2023 President’s Corner

This past week the Board of Directors Meeting in Palm Hill did not occur as we have come to expect, orderly and respectful. Going forward, we will follow our established guidelines for participating in the meeting. Written requests to speak on specific agenda topics must be sent to the Secretary, Bill Prior, Park Manager, Megan DiBello or me prior to the start of the meeting. Following adjournment of the meeting there will be an opportunity for shareholders to come to the microphone to make a statement. Each speaker may speak for up to 3 minutes when called to the microphone. Disorderliness or disruptive behavior will not be tolerated. We have to do better.

Carmen Foti brought up Robert’s Rules of Order regarding how the chair is supposed to vote in the meeting. We are considered a small board and as such, Rule 4.56 in the 12th edition of Robert’s Rules of Order has been the practice where the chair simply has one vote as a director, as does each of the other directors.

25 hours ahead of the meeting I received an invalid petitionfrom Staples that appears to have been sent by Cheryl Sienko to Megan DiBello and me after being forwarded an hour earlier from Staples to Dan Hannafin, Carmen Foti, Kris Schneider and Cheryl Sienko. 107 names, some illegible, appear on these papers. 15 names so far have been identified as dual signatures from the same addresses. The list is being further reviewed to determine any other fraudulent names. The petition had misspellings, including Kris Schneider’s name, as it was promoting her for treasurer. This document is being further analyzed to determine who Staples invoiced and how they were paid.

Carmen Foti brought up the US Government’s Paycheck Protection Program (PPP,) in which the Board voted to accept $66,472.31 of PPP Funds from Resource Property Management (RPM) at its March 20, 2023 board meeting. Upon the advice of RPM and our legal counsel, the funds will be held in a CD (currently 5.3%) until October 2027, at which time these funds can become income.

Continue to provide your input by contacting committee and board members, assisting on projects for the betterment of Palm Hill and assisting neighbors through our many avenues for volunteering. Working together, we can keep Palm Hill strong!

Pat Foor, President
Homeowners of Palm Hill, Inc.
