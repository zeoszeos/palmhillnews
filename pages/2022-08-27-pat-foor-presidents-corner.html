President’s Corner

Aug, 2022

As fall arrives and we prepare for the winter season I thought I would take this opportunity to bring shareholders up to date on a few items of general interest.

First, a thank you to Chuck Silcox, Stewart Ashton, Chuck Obi and Dan Hannafin for their hard work to set up our live Board Meeting this past week that also ran on Zoom and Channel 732. While the meeting was on Zoom, it is also the first Board meeting where we have had Board members and shareholders participating in the Clubhouse since Mar, 2020, when the pandemic dictated that we rely exclusively on Zoom style meetings. We will continue to try to improve the audio quality for future meetings. Also, thank you to Teri Bach and Nancy Stultz, who handled badge checks and John Brissa, who served as Sergeant at Arms. Contact Rich or Megan at the office or me with questions that you have and we will try to respond timely to you.

If you did not tune in to the Aug 24th Board meeting to listen yourself or view it on the recording, I think that all Shareholders should take comfort in the good order of our finances. A special thanks to our Treasurer, Dave Kohli, the Finance Committee, and the Management Company for their diligent work in managing our finances. We are are operating under budget for the year so far and have healthy surpluses in our operating, savings, and reserve accounts.

On particular matters, the increase in RV Parking Spaces Project has been in the planning phase for several months. The City of Largo and engineering services through Deuel and Associates are an integral part of the planning team to assure we meet all requirements as we proceed. This project will benefit all residents as it will provide for more RV, trailer, and boat parking and also produce a new revenue stream (Estimate of up to $17,000 per annum) to help offset Association expenses through the additional fees generated from the new rental parking spaces. We should have a good return on our investment and recapture costs within three to five years. Our governing documents give us the authority to undertake this project as determined by the Board and found in the Prospectus at paragraph 6.C. (pages 20-21 of the Circular.) In italics below is the applicable authorizing text from the last sentence of Paragraph 6.C.:

Specifically, “The Association may increase or decrease the size or modify the use of any of the facilities to serve the changing needs of the park as determined by the Association.” Along with other named facilities and improvements in this paragraph the “Recreational Vehicle/Boat Storage Area” is named as one of the facilities that may be increased.

The funds for this project are coming from our savings account and future revenues collected will go into the operating budget. Look for updates as this project develops.

Also, our Board approved a monthly food distribution program for seniors that is being provided by Tampa Bay Harvest and quite ably organized and administered by Linda Keehl and her volunteers is proving quite helpful for about 100 residences. I am certain as inflation continues to eat away at food budgets that the program will prove even more useful. Please contact Linda or the Park office for information on the program.

Additionally, as you may know, we will be undertaking some significant infrastructure improvements beginning probably in Sep. This spring, the Board approved an expenditure of over $600,000 dollars from our reserve account to repair and replace dated underground sanitary sewer pipes in the south part of the park. Because of sand infiltration into our lift station and water pumps the south part of the park will be the first to have work done. Also, this is the first of a few projects that the Board is developing with the help of Deuel and Associates, our engineering firm, to address outdated sewer and stormwater infrastructure.

PLEASE NOTE: For the health and safety of our maintenance staff the following changes are being enforced with recycling pick up and disposal, effective immediately:

Only clear see-thru bags will be considered for the recycling dumpster. Any opaque bags will go into the trash dumpster. If non-recyclable material is seen in the see clear bag the entire bag goes into the trash dumpster (such as adult diapers, food or other non-recyclable materials.)

Our recycling program does save us money but we must follow Largo’s rules to not mix in any non-recyclable materials or we are charged as trash for the entire dumpster. Details for this program are found at the back of the LINK. Thank you for your participation in this cost saving program that is good for the environment.

We are entering the season where severe weather is possible so be prepared by knowing how you will respond to protect yourself and your home in the event of a weather emergency. The Manager’s Corner in the LINK provides updated details for Palm Hill residents. Have your plan and be ready to act on short notice.

Pat Foor, President
Home Owners of Palm Hill, Inc.
