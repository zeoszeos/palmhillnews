President’s Corner

Oct, 2022

On behalf of the Board of Directors I would like to express our heartfelt thank you’s to all who assisted during the lead up to Hurricane Ian’s striking and in the aftermath with cleanup efforts. Residents and staff continue to assist those who need help and this reinforces what a great caring park we live in. It takes time to get everything done and your patience and assistance are greatly appreciated.

Please be mindful of what happens in manufactured home parks as seen on last week’s videos of southwest Florida. The evacuation order is given to protect you as you leave for a safer location. Make alternate plans early, prepare your home to the extent possible (awnings down, small items in yard and porch taken inside, etc.) Your safety is most important during a critical time such as last week. After the evacuation order is given, no special emergency assistance will be provided in the park by the local authorities.

We were very fortunate to avoid the full wrath of Ian this time but we must be prepared for potential storm events in the future. By taking care of each other we’ll keep Palm Hill the warm and friendly place we all call home.

We currently have two areas of concern with our financial budget. Our water bills for the North section of our park are much higher than projected. We have a volunteer team, led by Chuck Silcox, seeking to find the source of this problem. They are looking at flow issues as well as the new meters that were installed on our system. Shut off valves are being repaired and added so that smaller sections of homes in the park may be shut down when a leak occurs. As seasonal residents return we will again be asking you to check your toilets for possible leaks to be repaired. We must try to contain this expense to the best of our ability.

Another area of financial concern is in the refuse center with our construction dumpsters. They are clearly marked to not fill past the top line of the dumpster as the city refuses to empty them until the over the top items have been removed, which falls to our maintenance team to correct. Costs continue to rise for our heavy usage and a solution must be found to prevent abuse in this area. Common courtesy is requested as you use this area. If an entire home is being remodeled at one time please order a dumpster for your driveway during the process to remove construction materials. This construction dumpster is provided for the shareholder who is doing a project within the unit to properly dispose of removed materials. Thank you for your cooperation as we determine next steps for this area.

Please contact me to share your input on these or other topics as we work together for Palm Hill.

Pat Foor, President
Homeowners of Palm Hill, Inc.
