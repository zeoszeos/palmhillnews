Earlier this month our Board of Directors Treasurer, Dan Avery, changed his status here in Palm Hill from shareholder to renter. This change in his status necessitated him to resign his position on our Board of Directors, as he is not a shareholder at present. We are grateful to Dan for his tireless service to our Board of Directors and we look forward to his and Gail's return as renters next season.

Dan's departure leaves us with an opening on our Board of Directors that will be an agenda item at our May 31st Board meeting.

Some shareholders have inquired about the process for filling this open Board of Directors position between now and the January, 2024 annual election. Article IV.4.1 of our Bylaws addresses this situation. It provides that the departure by resignation of a Board Member, or a Board Member who is no longer a shareholder, creates a vacant seat. Then Section 4.2.5 of our Bylaws applies.

Section 4.2.5 gives the Board of Directors the right to appoint, by majority vote of the remaining Directors, a person to temporarily fill the vacancy created. With eight Directors remaining, a majority vote is five(5.)

Any person who fills a vacancy under this provision will end their term of service at the next annual election. Then a person will be elected by the membership to fill the remainder of the unexpired term left by Dan's resignation (one year.)

We welcome all shareholders at our Board of Directors meetings, where the business of Homowners of Palm Hill, Inc. is being conducted.

Thank you,

Pat Foor, President
Homeowners of Palm Hill, Inc.
