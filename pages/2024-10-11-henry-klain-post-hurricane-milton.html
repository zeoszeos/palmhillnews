Friday, Oct 11, 2024Listen

Dear fellow Palm Hillers:

We are extremely fortunate and blessed that residents responded so favorably in evacuating Palm Hill. Thankfully, there were no injuries that I am aware of, and now we can focus on overcoming the challenges that we face individually and as a community.

Our Park Manager, Megan, will lead communications with respect to the status of all Palm Hill infrastructure. The cataloguing of damage began yesterday morning and will be completed in the next day or so. Please be assured that the office will contact all homeowners as soon as possible if their house shows exterior damage.

Your patience is appreciated as we work through the logistics. Our messaging will utilize email, Vantaca and Channel 732. While we are at the mercy of electricity and telecommunication outages, cellular services remain available for the most part.

I would like to recognize those of you currently in the park who have reached out to neighbors, checked on homes and offered assistance. On behalf of the Board, a heartfelt thank you!

Further information and direction will be forthcoming as it becomes available.

Palm Hill Strong!

Henry Klain
President
HOPH Inc

Sent to Palm Hill residents by email from Resource Property Management on behalf of Home Owners of Palm Hill, Inc on Friday, Oct 11, 2024, at 10:10 AM ET.
