🌴 Palm Hill Country Club

Communications Committee report — Aug 17

📅 Monday, August 17, 2026

  • Victoria Bruce will be stepping down as Communication Committee Chair, as she and her husband are selling their residence in Palm Hill. A new chair will need to be identified.

  • The committee welcomed Lisa Bria as the new editor of The Link.

  • Work continues on streamlining the process for uploading informational slides to the Community Channel on TV. The team will soon look into viewership data to help justify the ongoing effort.

  • Tony Ciaramitaro presented the new online amenity booking system, with plans to transition its administration to the Communication Committee.

  • Nearly all committee meeting minutes for 2025 and 2026 have been uploaded to the Palm Hill website, under the "HOPH Information" section.

  • A sub-committee has been formed to begin work on Candidates Night.

  • The team continues to produce the weekly e-newsletter and is working to streamline distribution procedures. With four issues now distributed, the committee is requesting from the Board formal approval to continue production.
