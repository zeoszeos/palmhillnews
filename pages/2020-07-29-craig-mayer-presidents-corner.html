The President's Corner

This message discusses a few matters pertaining to the governance of the Park. It is being provided so that shareholders have credible and valid information on the topics and are not misled by or succumb to disinformation or wild and unfounded rumors that appear to be circulating in social media and elsewhere.

The Park Manager’s Resignation. On Wednesday 22 Jul the Executive Committee met to consider the recently received resignation letter from our Park Manager, Mr. Rich Thomas and to discuss with him the workload and pressures he has experienced in performing the duties of his position. On that matter, and I am sure after careful consideration by Mr. Thomas, I am very pleased to report that Mr. Thomas has decided to rescind his resignation and continue as the Park Manager.

I am also pleased to report that the Executive Committee has authorized the hiring of an assistant Park Manager which will add one person and a new position to the office staff. To make a long story short, Mr. Thomas has been unduly pressed and indeed overwhelmed with the workload that the business experiences and this has been the case for a very long time. Of course, these pressures have been increased by the Covid-19 situation which will be with us for the indefinite future. Please keep in mind that the Association is a five-million dollar per year for-profit corporate enterprise. The management responsibilities are demanding, varied, and extensive.

Mr. Thomas’s dedication to his profession and to Palm Hill and our residents is laudable and we as a community are quite fortunate to have him as our Park Manager - particularly now, as we all are challenged with the fears and uncertainties of the Covid-19 pandemic.

The Jackson Lawsuit. As most shareholders are aware, the Association was sued last year by one of our residents with respect to a personal injury matter. The case remains in the discovery stage and is progressing slowly for various reasons I am sure, not the least of which are the adjustments the judicial systems have had to make as a result of the Covid-19 crisis. In any event, please know that the lawsuit poses absolutely no threat or diminishment to the financial status or well-being of the Association, which is quite sound. The suit is being managed by legal counsel appointed by the insurance company with the insurance company under the insurance policy having full authority to manage and resolve the lawsuit.

Executive Committee Meetings and Actions: On this topic I want to reassure shareholders, contrary to assertions and claims that are appearing in social media, that meetings and actions by the Association’s Executive Committee are entirely legal and in full accord and compliance with the Bylaws. Please excuse some of the thickly legal discussion on this subject, but it is necessary to an understanding of the Committee’s role and authority.

Paragraph 4.13 of our bylaws specifically empowers the Committee to “…have…and…exercise all of the powers of the Board of Directors in management of the business and affairs of the cooperative during the intervals between the meetings of the board of directors insofar as may be permitted by law….” The Bylaw then excepts from the committee’s authority the power to establish the budget, determine assessments, or amend or adopt rules governing the details of the operation or use of the cooperative property.”

The “insofar as may be permitted by law” phrase in the Bylaws refers to Florida Statutes Chapter 607.0825 (4) (a) thru (d). This provision specifies that any “board committee” which includes an executive committee like ours may not: 1) authorize or approve the reacquisition of shares …, 2) approve, recommend, or propose to shareholders actions required by Chapter 607 to be approved by shareholders…,3) fill vacancies on the board of directors or any board committee…,and 4) adopt, amend, or repeal bylaws. Please note that the term “board committee” as used under the statute means committees that may exercise powers of the board. At Palm Hill, except for the Executive Committee all committees are advisory and may not exercise powers of the board.

Since the past election the Executive Committee has met on four (4) occasions – Mar 2nd, Mar 20th, Jul 6th, and Jul 22nd. With the exception of the Mar 20th meeting which dealt with what can fairly be described as very urgent insurance matters, all of the meetings have dealt with personnel or legal matters. The Committee has considered and acted upon only matters properly within the authority of the Committee. Also, since the election there have been seven (7) Board of Director meetings. Minutes of Board and Executive Committee meetings appear in the HOPH website information page.

None of the agenda items in the most recent Jul 22nd Executive Committee meeting were or could have been noticed or included in the cancelled board meeting that was set for Jul 15th. With respect to that meeting or its rescheduling, if you will, after discussion among Board members a date will be set for holding a meeting of the Board in the near future. Consideration of various bids and authorization to commence repairs to the lift station will be included in the meeting. That was the key item on the recent agenda, but it became apparent that bids and proposals were not in place to allow for the Board to properly consider the project(s).

In researching the business practices and history of the Association It was unusual to find that there were no minutes of or references to executive committee activity in the course of the past seven years. In a corporation as substantial as that of Palm Hill, the executive committee normally plays an active and important role.

For example, and certainly in my experience, the Committee often doubles as the personnel committee or has responsibility for personnel matters. It takes action or makes recommendations to the Board as appropriate. Personnel issues as well as other matters such as, the sudden appearance of unauthorized Park occupants or approval of new house plans, infrequently time themselves to be addressed only at prescheduled Board meetings. The executive committee has an important role to play and should and can be used as envisioned by the governing documents.

Covid-19 Emergency: In the way of a reminder, the comprehensive updated protocols the Board and Park Manager put in place and published on Jun 8, 2020 remain in effect. They were modified in late Jun, in accord with Pinellas County mandates, with the requirement for the wearing of masks when indoors. Their rules, not ours, but adopted by Palm Hill.

While we all hear of the progress being made with treatments and vaccines the virus remains an unpredictable, fast moving, and lethal threat. With the explosion of Covid-19 cases in Florida it would appear that authorities will maintain their current guidelines and rules, or possibly tighten them, until infection rates and numbers decrease. And we will follow.

For everyone wherever you may be living at the moment, please practice social distancing and please wear masks. Thank you for abiding the Park protocols, and Thank you for supporting Palm Hill.

Craig Mayer
President
HOPH, Inc.
