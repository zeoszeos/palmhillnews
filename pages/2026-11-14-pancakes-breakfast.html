Pancakes Breakfast

Saturday, November 14, 2026 · 8:30 AM to 10:00 AM · North Rec Hall

Come join your friends and neighbors in the North Rec Hall for a delicious pancake breakfast, including three pancakes, two sausages, applesauce, orange juice, and coffee or tea. Tickets will be sold at Monday and Wednesday coffees by Carolyn Hooper, or you may call her at (727)518-0330; $4 in advance, $5 at the door. Come join us and enjoy a great pancake breakfast.
