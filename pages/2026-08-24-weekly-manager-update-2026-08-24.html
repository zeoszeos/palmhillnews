Weekly Manager Update

📅 Monday, August 24, 2026

  • Attached is information from CAI regarding the Corporate Transparency Act. For those of you who were on the Board when all of this was being reviewed, it has been determined that beneficial ownership information need not be reported for US companies and individuals. I've attached the email received from CAI on the matter for you to read.

  • At our corporate office, they track all the Association's meeting dates to ensure that everyone hits their mailing deadlines. The meeting dates submitted for Palm Hill are: Annual Meeting (1/20/2027) and Budget Approval (3/17/2027). These dates mean that the mailings for the annual meeting have deadlines of 11/20/2026 & 12/21/2026. The budget needs to be mailed to shareholders on or before 2/12/2027 due to the President's Day holiday, which falls on the actual deadline of 2/15/2027. Please let me know if any of you have any concerns about these dates.

  • There is an issue with the variable frequency drive that was installed at the south well. In the various discussions I've had, the vendor, in consultation with the manufacturer, determined that the drive was defective and is replacing it under warranty.

  • There was a lightning strike to the pine tree in the green space located at 12 Pindo Palm St. E. We called in BrightView for emergency service to remove what was left since it was extremely damaged and in danger of toppling over. Picture attached. Additionally, it broke the windows at two homes, so we are working to replace those.

  • Address numbers have been installed on the south clubhouse on both ends of the building to aid people in locating the building and package delivery. This is in addition to the new canopy in the north, which has the address number on it. See attached picture.

  • Attached is a notice that has been modified to fit Palm Hill. If there are no objections, we will send this out to residents next week.

  • There were a couple of minor deficiencies noted in the fire alarm and hydrant inspection earlier this month. We have approved a new kit to address a hydrant that is not opening smoothly. Maintenance is installing new batteries in two locations. They have also provided a quote to replace a speaker strobe light in the north clubhouse.

  • The issue with the air conditioner in the north clubhouse reoccurred, so the coil and pan are being replaced in the unit. The parts are still under warranty, so we will pay only for labor. Additionally, they will be installing a new return plenum for this unit.

  • Grease traps were pumped out this week by Wind River. This occurs 3 times yearly to remain compliant with the City of Largo.

  • The quote for the transformer was received, which is $26,680. This will be included in the agenda for the Board meeting. I've asked Sean what amount Transform is willing to contribute towards this.
