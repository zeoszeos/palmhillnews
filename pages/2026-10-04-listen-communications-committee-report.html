Communications Committee Report

Tap the speaker or Read Report Aloud to begin.

Tony Ciaramitaro chaired his first meeting as the committee's new chair, with all nine members present. The August 17 minutes were approved as corrected.

Webmaster: 174 email blasts have gone out to 1,357 addresses since the June 8 meeting.

The LINK: the committee is looking at simple ways to get reader feedback through the LINK distributors.

Community Channel: all Board and committee meeting recordings have been uploaded except the August 17 Communications meeting, which was too large for the upload limit.

Booking system: the new online booking system and calendar go live October 1. The committee will put out a short notice to residents before the switch.

e-Newsletter: Steve Leonowicz walked the committee through the weekly e-newsletter. Committee chairs are encouraged to send meeting summaries to palmhillblast@gmail.com.

Facebook: the committee voted 7 to 2 to resume the association's Facebook page on a two-month trial as a one-way channel, with comments turned off and posting limited to Jean Stjernstrom, Gail Stott and Tony Ciaramitaro. The Board's 2021 content policy still applies, and Nextdoor stays off. The chair will report back at the end of the trial.

Candidates Night: Elaine Winegar, Lisa Bria, Gail Stott and Tony Ciaramitaro formed a working group and set its first planning meeting for September 23. After the meeting, Candidates Night was scheduled for Wednesday, January 6, 2027, and a survey was sent to past Board candidates.

Committee-run Zoom training was discussed to ease the load on Zoom volunteers. No decision was made; the chair will return with a proposal.

Next meeting: Monday, October 12, 2026 at 10:00 AM, South Clubhouse Boardroom and Zoom.
