A Message from Board President Craig Mayer

Presidents Corner - August 30, 2020

This message might best be described as a status report as well as a bit of a pre-season forecast as summer ends, seasonal residents begin to return to their Palm Hill homes, and we approach the resumption of the winter season. Herein are a number of topics of interest for shareholders which include the Board’s recent personnel and staffing meeting on August 25th , the Olinger lawsuit, and a few other news worthy items.

The 25 August 2020 Board meeting: At the meeting the Board confirmed the Assistant Manager position and approved the hiring of Ms. Megan DiBello to fill the position. Ms. DiBello has worked for Resource Property Management (RPM) for several years, has extensive administrative and management experience, is licensed as a Certified Association Manager (CAM), and has a bachelor degree from the University of South Florida. We are fortunate to have someone with her expertise joining our staff. Please extend a warm welcome to her as she joins the Palm Hill community.

The Board also discussed overall workforce needs. To understand these needs it is necessary to first explain the past and current overall staffing situation at Palm Hill and likely future requirements. These matters and workforce needs, of course, are rather fluid at this time and have all been influenced by the Covid-19 emergency.

We have fifteen (15) staff positions at Palm Hill to include the Park Manager and new Assistant Manager positions. Of these fifteen, five (5) are unfilled. These are: one part-time (1) janitorial position for the north clubhouse and area, three (3) part-time pro-shop staff positions, and one full-time (1) front office administrative assistant position (Emily’s former position). All of these positions, including the costs associated with the new Assistant Manager position are covered in the budget. To make a longer story short, the new Assistant Manager position will not be resulting in added costs to Palm Hill – rather, it will result in enhancing the quality and efficiency of our management staff.

The part-time staffing plan for the pro-shop was approved by the Board at the March 18, 2020 board meeting. It was necessitated by the retirement of Bonnie Roberts in March 2020 who held a full-time position as pro-shop manager for many, many years. However, due to the Covid-19 emergency and the resulting circumstance of fewer players and activities the pro-shop positions have not been filled. They will only be filled as the needs dictate and most likely in phases.

Similarly, due to reduced facility usage the need to fill the janitorial position immediately for the north clubhouse is not pressing as its re-opening is currently being delayed.

Regarding the open full-time administrative assistant position, the Park Manager believes and recommends that the position can remain unfilled for the indefinite future and that if it is required to be filled it would be done so as a part-time position. The Assistant Manager will likely be taking a lead role in real estate transaction matters and the duties and requirements for this administrative position will be re-evaluated in the months ahead.

The Olinger lawsuit: I am pleased to report that HOPH, Inc. prevailed at the hearing held on August 12th where the Olinger’s were seeking to vacate the court order approving the settlement agreement entered in June 2019. The judge denied the Olinger’s motion to vacate or escape the agreement. As a result of the ruling the Association is entitled to seek recoupment of legal fees. You may also recall that this is the case where Mr. Hanke, one of the directors, was actively assisting the Olingers and, in effect, became an opposing party in the lawsuit. The Association had to incur legal expenses responding to document inquiries from Mr. Hanke’s lawyer who is also the Olinger’s lawyer. While successful, we have still incurred $15,000 in legal expenses on just this most recent activity in the case.

Other News worthy items: The tar-sealing of Park roads was completed in August and by all reports our contractor did an excellent job. On behalf of the Board I would like to thank all the residents for their patience and understanding as the work progressed. Also, a note of thanks to Ms. Debra Geer, the Park Manager, and the Building and Grounds Committee for all the hard work and time they devoted to seeing the project organized and completed.

The resumption of the palm tree planting project approved earlier this year by the Board will be getting underway on Tuesday, September 1st and should be completed in two or three days.

The Association showed a $20,000 profit for the month of July which is the first month of the new fiscal year. Our financial condition is quite sound and our reserve accounts remain fully funded.

Covid-19: Since those sobering days of late March we have experienced only a small number of additional Covid-19 cases in the Park. And, we have no reports of any additional fatalities or serious health consequences involving our residents. Moreover, we have been faithfully served by our on-site staff throughout the emergency and that service continues. While we have had a few scares no member of our workforce has come down with Covid-19. This is a testament to their regard for their own and others’ health and safety. Please thank them when you can.

The phased reopening of Park facilities and amenities is working well and will continue with Mr. Thomas managing the protocols. On behalf of the Board I would like to thank all the residents for their attention to CDC guidelines, their own and, in turn, each other’s safety. A thanks also to those who are being extra careful in hosting and enjoying the recently resumed Wednesday morning coffees and Thursday bingos.

On a personal note, I just finished reading the 2004 novel “The Great Influenza.” It chronicles the heartbreaking experience the world endured over a hundred years ago with the “Spanish Flu” pandemic of 1918. One may as well have been reading yesterday’s headlines. The Covid-19 Pandemic is far from being over or contained. It remains a deadly and ever-present threat. Please be vigilant. Social distance, wear masks, and avoid social gatherings – particularly indoors.

More unsolicited emails. Regrettably, I must again address improper actions by two board members. First however a disclaimer is required. Specifically, the email blasts sent out to shareholders by Directors Hanke and Foti on Saturday 22 August regarding the 25 August Board meeting were not authorized or sanctioned by the Board of Directors nor transmitted over official communication channels. Further, the email addresses were not released by the Association for use by these directors.

Rather, they were taken from the Member Directory on Palm Hill’s website in violation of the website ’s Terms of Use and first used by Mr. Foti in his failed effort to influence the outcome of the 2020 Board election. You may recall that this matter, as well as possible voting irregularities and misuse of Association letterhead were the subject of a special investigation conducted last February. It is disturbing that two Palm Hill Board members would knowingly violate our website’s expressed protection of personal email addresses from being harvested en masse and used for unsolicited communications.

Please understand that the unsolicited emails from Director Foti and now Hanke are sent in pursuit of their personal political agenda – they do not represent anything at all official or sanctioned by the Board. They should not be sent at all in the conduct of board business and, not surprisingly, are typically filled with distortions, errors, and misrepresentations that can alarm and mislead shareholders

Thank You for Supporting Palm Hill,

Craig Mayer
President,
HOPH, Inc.
