Jun 3, 2021

It has been a little while since my last report, but a few issues appear to need some clarification.

I have not reported the results of the last two board meetings because there are several ways for residents to stay informed, including attending meetings via zoom or at the north club house, watching channel 732 on TV, viewing past meetings or looking up the minutes and motions on the website. We are pleased with the way these various forms of communication have worked out.

Mesco Operations

To be very clear, neither the Board nor the Executive Committee had anything whatsoever to do with the folks who run Mesco cancelling their services. They did this of their own choosing.

To the best of my knowledge, a previous board likely approved Mesco storing their equipment in one of HOPH’s sheds and operating the service they provide. It would make sense that Mesco asked for this approval as a Corporation, and with the understanding that they would secure their own insurance, to minimize the liability the park would be exposed to.

It is my understanding that by not updating their corporate status on an annual basis as required, Mesco allowed their corporate status to lapse, which I believe meant they could no longer secure insurance for their operation. Based on the personal liability they might be exposed to, they decided to shut down.

It is my personal view (not necessarily that of all board members) that Mesco now has three options:

1. Re-instate Corporate Status – The folks that run Mesco could apply to the proper authority to re-instate their corporate status. By doing so I believe they could obtain the insurance they have always had and carry on their operations as they have done in the past.

2. Operate under the Umbrella of the South Rec Club – it seems possible that Mesco could ask to operate their services as part of the South Rec Club, who is a corporate entity, and who has already secured the insurance it requires. There may be an additional cost, but it might be less than securing separate insurance.

3. Board Involvement: If neither of the two above options work for Mesco, then they would need a board member to make a motion at a future board meeting for whatever decision or support they are seeking. However, it must be understood that in considering any such motion, the board would have to satisfy itself about any new liability they might be exposing the park to and decide accordingly.

On-Line Voting – Implementation of the system is progressing, and you will hear more about it shortly.

Reserve Study and Infrastructure Replacement plans - A preliminary Reserve Study has been received and will be reviewed by the Finance Committee, and our Engineer is preparing a detailed and phased in infrastructure replacement plan. My approach is to incorporate specific summarized costs from the engineer’s detailed report into a Revised Palm Hill Prepared Reserve Study (in accordance with Florida Statutes) that is in line with what we can afford to do, and over a time period that reflects reasonable increases in our contributions to the Reserve Fund.

Pickleball and Bocci Courts – The Planning Committee is studying the possibility of building 3 new pickleball courts, including the moving and rebuilding of 2 bocci courts that are in serious need of upgrade. Through the open meeting process several options are being talked about and some budgeted costs are being discussed. The committee has raised several concerns that are being looked into.

Please remember that this committee has no authority whatsoever to decide something for the park or spend money. Its only course of action is to make recommendations to the board. If you would like to make a contribution to the debate please do so, but I beg that you do so in a constructive respectful manner, being clear about whether or not you support it and why, and/or offering any other alternatives.

I hope the above clears up some of the comments going around the park these days.

Thanks for your support.

Dave Kohli, President
