This message is from Craig Mayer:

The President’s Corner

Dear Palm Hill Residents

On behalf of the Board of Directors I would like to take this opportunity to address the community as we begin a new year. This is the first message of what we intend to publish periodically, perhaps once every few months and as otherwise necessary, where the President or another board officer or committee chair can discuss events or projects impacting Palm Hill and keep residents better informed.

These messages will supplement the “Editor’s Corner” and the “Manager’s Corner” which appear in the Link. Soon, they will also be appearing on the TV and Website. The Board believes that improving our communications to residents is quite important.

The Election: As you are probably aware, we had a very spirited Board of Directors election season and it was capped with a recent record turnout of approximately 630 ballots being cast at the annual/election meeting on Jan 22nd.

The Board would like to thank everyone who assisted in support of the election such as Annie Holtby and her group of election ballot inspectors and vote counters for contributing their valuable time and energies to helping us manage our mini-democracy. Thanks also to all the candidates for stepping forward to offer their time and talents to the benefit of Palm Hill. Also, kudos to our management company, Resource Property Management and Dot Thomas; to our Park Manager, Rich Thomas; and to the office staff, Debra Geer and Emily Eakins for their tireless efforts and oversight in helping us hold the election.

Current issues re: Email Addresses

Shareholders have wanted to know how a Board member obtained email addresses the shareholders thought were private, and in some cases, marked to be hidden from view on the Member Directory.

Moreover, the Board member’s election period group emails were never authorized by or condoned by the Board.

A website administrator took steps to hide all emails until the workings of the privacy parameters on the Member Directory could be examined, and any other access points to shareholders’ information were checked.

Any organizational communications system(s) should be examined on a regular basis to ensure parameters are in place to prevent misuse or abuse, and that the system is functioning to meet the needs of the members of that organization.

When a problem occurs, immediate attention to that communications system is needed. The administrators of that system have authority to act. And the organization has a responsibility to its members to check into the problems and create a solution going forward.

For example, when a company has a breach of its customers’ information, immediate safeguards are put in place until the breach can be investigated and the problems fixed. If some functions of the computer system are temporarily halted to ensure the safety of the entire system, then that must be done, and it is incumbent upon those administering that system to do so, despite the inconvenience to customers while the problem is being solved.

The process and reason for the veiled emails were briefly explained on the website Message Board, but the Message Board does not reach all members of the Palm Hill Community and is not the official channel of verified communication from the Palm Hill Owners Association, Inc. Thus, this group email was deemed necessary.

Next Steps about the email listings and privacy:

In the coming weeks, an authorized website administrator will unveil (restore) all the shareholder emails – except those that were already listed as hidden. You will be advised when that happens.

All shareholders are then urged to log in to the system, look at their profile information, and then choose whether they wish to hide their personal information or allow it to be viewed by all who have access to the Member Directory. All should check the status of their information. Instructions will be provided at that time.

Note: Hiding the information will not affect the shareholders’ ability to receive the group emails sent to all shareholders. But because the group emails go out via the emails listed on the website system, a shareholder must have a profile on the website.

Those who do not hide their information should understand that anyone who has access to the Member Directory (only Palm Hill residents) can copy their information and use it. This is very similar to when a person’s name, address and phone number were listed in the public White Pages published by the phone company.

Upcoming Meetings: The first Board of Directors meeting with the new Board will be held in the North Clubhouse on Feb 5th at 3 PM. The following Board meeting will be on the 26th of Feb, also at 3 PM in the North clubhouse. Meetings for Mar are planned to be held in the South clubhouse. The Board intends to publish the meeting agenda as far in advance of the meeting as possible in order for residents to know the agenda and to have the opportunity to speak on agenda issues if they care to.

Thank You

Craig L. Mayer
President, HOPH, Inc.
